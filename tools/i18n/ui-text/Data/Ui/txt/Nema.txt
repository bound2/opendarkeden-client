Thirteen pages left by Lilith, mother of all Vampires, at her death, setting down magic power and the laws of the Vampires. They have been handed down through the generations to the twelve Vampire Masters, and it is said that gathering all 12 Bible fragments reveals where the 13th, the Blood Bible, was left. Whoever obtains that final Bible is said to come close to the magic of immortality and the secret of eternal life.
