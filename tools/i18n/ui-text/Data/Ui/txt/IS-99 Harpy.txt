I&S's new sniper rifle, the Harpy, applies the latest technology of the 1990s, so it is light and powerful. Its precision shooting is also excellent, but overall it rates as a mid-range SR. The IS-99's metal surfaces are finished in dark blue, and it is designed so that parts such as the AV-NV special lens for night fighting against Vampires and the chrome-lined barrel can be swapped at will. The stock is coated in epoxy resin. It once drew the Slayers' attention because the elite sniper Jean was said to favor it.
