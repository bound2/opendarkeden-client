A soldier's mission is to neutralize important targets. But shooting is not aimed only at living targets (people). In fact, power stations, radar bases, radio relay stations and the like are targeted more often than people. So soldiers must be able to show wit and adaptability in any situation. A high-performance SMG is also essential. For troops, a high-performance semi-automatic SMG that can protect them in enemy territory is the right choice.
Based on this concept and on combat experience, H&K developed the MG-90 'Shylph' SMG. The MG-90 'Shylph' is operated by bolt recoil (bolt action). It uses a 600mm barrel and a single-stage trigger system (the trigger pressure stays the same from start to firing), and the trigger length can be adjusted up and down. The stock length and cheek-piece height can also be adjusted. It uses a 6X telescopic lens system called STANG, with separate versions for night and day.
