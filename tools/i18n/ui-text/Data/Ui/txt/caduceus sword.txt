Found in an Islamic mosque, it has two snakes carved on its grip. It is believed to have belonged to a member of the Assassins, the secret order of killers based in the fortress of Alamut in the mountains of northern Iran. To revive the order, they are known to have seized an Islamic mosque in India and used it as a base for political assassination plots.
