< file = 'HelpMessage_17' pos = 'L'>

< file = 'HelpMessage_18' pos = 'L'>
   (5) Guild chat (Ctrl+G) - Talk only with your guild members.
< file = 'HelpMessage_19' pos = 'L'>
   (6) Chat On/Off - Click the bead-shaped icon to turn that chat on or off. Red means the chat is active, and black means it is off. When you turn a chat off, none of the other players' messages in it are shown.

* You can also click the icons in the chat window to use that chat.

<#color ='80ff80' a = 'y'>
1) Race traits
<<#color ='ffffff' a = 'y'>

Slayers grow the Domain level of their class by repeatedly performing actions and skills with weapons of that Domain; growth is based on skill (proficiency).
What sets the game apart from others is its completely distinct character in its growth system, items, skills and classes. For travel, there are Motorcycles and Helicopters.
In war, Slayers are not especially strong one on one, but when the classes band together, there is no enemy they need fear.
One of DKx Online's systems changes stats with the time of day. Day and night do not just change your field of view or the brightness of the scenery; they change each race's stats. At night Slayers look somewhat weak against the Vampires, but by day they are relatively strong.


<#color ='80ff80' a = 'y'>
2) Slayer classes
<<#color ='ffffff' a = 'y'>

A Slayer can master a single Domain or learn two or more Domains at once. However, since the total of your Domain levels is capped, keep in mind that you cannot learn every Domain to 100%, and that once you hit the cap, raising one skill Domain makes another Domain decline.
As your Domain levels grow, you can learn new skills. As a skill's level rises, its duration or Damage increases. Skills only level up if you keep using them, and a skill's level is limited by your Domain level.
---------------------------------------------------------
- Domain levels and skill levels are both divided into 5 grades by level
Grade   Level         Grade
1      1~24          Apprentice
2      25~49         Adept
3      50~74         Expert
4      75~99         Master
5      100~ 150      Grand Master
--------------------------------------------------------

<#color ='4080ff' a = 'y'>
Tip> If your Domain level is Apprentice, you can raise your skill level up to Adept. If your Domain level is Adept, you can raise your skill level up to Expert. In other words, a skill's level can be raised to one grade above your Domain level.
<<#color ='ffffff' a = 'y'>



<#color ='80ff80' a = 'y'>
3) Domain traits
<<#color ='ffffff' a = 'y'>


<#color ='4080ff' a = 'y'>
o Warriors (Blade Domain, Sword Domain)
<<#color ='ffffff' a = 'y'>

A fighting branch that wields all kinds of swords and blades freely and specializes in close hand-to-hand combat. It splits into two lines: sword Slayers, who balance attack and defense, and blade Slayers, who boast tremendous strength and destructive power.
A branch that fights bravely at the head of the party, protecting its comrades; training STR has a great effect on its fighting power.

<#color ='4080ff' a = 'y'>
o Soldiers (Gun Domain)
<<#color ='ffffff' a = 'y'>

A branch that specializes in all kinds of firearms, explosives and the like, the products of modern, advanced military technology.
With covering fire from long range and mines laid on the enemy's likely approach routes, a soldier's mere presence in the party multiplies its operating range several times over. DEX has a large effect on the power and accuracy of its attacks.

<#color ='4080ff' a = 'y'>
o Clerics (Enchant Domain, Heal Domain)
<<#color ='ffffff' a = 'y'>

A special branch that uses the healing powers handed down in secret by various religions.
They are dedicated to healing and supporting the fighters. They are known to split into the Heal line, which can fully restore party members who have been put out of action, and the Enchant line, which maximizes party members' abilities and detects and foresees the evil arts of the Vampires and others. Long hours of combat support call for training INT.


<#color ='80ff80' a = 'y'>
4) Stat system (table)
<<#color ='ffffff' a = 'y'>


<#color ='4080ff' a = 'y'>
o Base stats
<<#color ='ffffff' a = 'y'>

- Base stats represent a character's basic yet important traits, and at the same time they set the range of the secondary stats.
* STR (STR): A stat for the character's strength and physical toughness; it affects HP, Damage, Protection and so on.
* DEX (DEX) : A stat for the character's quickness and agility; it affects To Hit, Defense, attack speed and so on.
* INT (INT) : A stat for the character's intellectual ability; it affects spell duration, magic Damage and so on.


<#color ='80ff80' a = 'y'>
5) Secondary stats of each race
<<#color ='ffffff' a = 'y'>


<#color ='4080ff' a = 'y'>
o Secondary stats
<<#color ='ffffff' a = 'y'>

Secondary stats are determined by your base stats and items, and they have an absolute effect on everything your character does in the game, such as combat and magic.
* HP (HP) : A stat for the character's life force; when HP reaches 0, you die. HP depends on your stats and level.
* To Hit (TOHIT) : A stat for the accuracy of your attacks; it depends on DEX.
* Damage (DAMAGE) : A stat for the amount of harm you can deal an opponent; it depends on STR and INT.
* Defense (DEFENSE) : A stat for your defense against To Hit; it is increased by DEX.
* Protection (PROTECTION) : A stat for your defense against Damage; it is increased by STR.


<#color ='80ff80' a = 'y'>
6) Experience system
<<#color ='ffffff' a = 'y'>


<#color ='4080ff' a = 'y'>
o How to earn Experience
<<#color ='ffffff' a = 'y'>

- To grow the Domain level of your class, you must repeatedly perform actions and skills with weapons of that Domain.
- Rising Domain levels affect the success rate, Damage and so on of some techniques.
- As your Domain levels rise, you can learn new skills. The Experience you get also varies with which skills you use at each level.
- Slayers earn different Experience for hunting the same monster by day and by night. At night they earn about 1.5 times as much Experience.
- For each Domain, Slayers have a suitable weapon for their level. If you wield a weapon of a higher grade than the suitable one, you earn up to 120% Domain Experience. For details on suitable weapons, search the FAQ on the homepage.

o Experience system
<<#color ='ffffff' a = 'y'>

- How to earn Experience
Vampires level up by accumulating Experience. There are two ways for a Vampire to earn Experience.
The first is Experience from Blood Drain (BloodDrain), which earns you 70% of a monster's total Experience. The second is Experience from the last kill (LastKill), which earns you 30% of a monster's total Experience. When the Experience you accumulate this way reaches your goal Experience (GoalExp), you level up, and as you level up you can learn new Vampire magic.

There are three ways to cure it: summon a coffin with the Summon Casket skill, drain the blood of a monster or another race, or use a Serum, and the HolyDamage is lifted.

5) Experience system
<<#color ='ffffff' a = 'y'>


Ousters level up by accumulating Experience. Experience is earned at the moment of a monster kill (Kill), based on the Damage you dealt the monster and the monster's level.
When the Experience you accumulate this way reaches your goal Experience (GoalExp), you level up, and the Skill Points you get for leveling up let you improve the skills you have or learn new magic.


<#color ='80ffff' a = 'y'>
TIP. Skill Points spent on a skill are never reset. Every skill is mastered at level 30, and once mastered its Skill Points cannot be redistributed, so choose carefully.
<<#color ='ffffff' a = 'y'>


DKx Online also has a lair system that works like quests.
In a lair quest, many more monsters appear in a shorter time than in ordinary hunting, so make a habit of joining the quest whenever a lair opens while you are hunting.

1) Alignment system
<<#color ='ffffff' a = 'y'>

- Alignment is a stat that shows how good or evil a character is. It was introduced to cut down on hostile acts such as PK between members of the same race.
- Alignment rises or falls when you attack your own race; attacking other races does not change it.
- Attacking characters of a lower level than you makes the change much larger.
- Also, if you keep doing bad deeds in a row, your Alignment drops at a progressive rate.
- Alignment ranges from -10,000 to 10,000, and starts at +7,500.
- Killing monsters can raise your Alignment up to -2500; to raise it any further, you must attack characters with an evil Alignment.
- Donating money to an NPC can raise your Alignment up to -2500; to raise it any further, you must attack characters whose Alignment is Evil.
- As a penalty, players with an evil Alignment are rather more likely to drop items when they die, and they also take a larger Experience penalty.

2) Party system
<<#color ='ffffff' a = 'y'>

A small-community support system introduced to strengthen group play for each race; the bigger the party, the bigger the bonus. Joining and creating a party are both done with right Alt + clicking a player.

<#color ='4080ff' a = 'y'>
o How Experience is shared
<<#color ='ffffff' a = 'y'>

When you or another party member attacks an enemy, the Experience, boosted according to the number of party members within a certain distance, is automatically shared among the members in proportion to their levels; for Slayers, SlayerLevel is used as the level. Here, party size means not the total number of people who joined the party, but the total number of party members within 8 tiles of you.

<#color ='4080ff' a = 'y'>
o Bonus by party size
<<#color ='ffffff' a = 'y'>


<#color ='80ffff' a = 'y'>
2 party members: 150% Experience bonus
3 party members: 195% Experience bonus
4 party members: 225% Experience bonus
5 party members: 250% Experience bonus
6 party members: 270% Experience bonus
<<#color ='ffffff' a = 'y'>


<#color ='4080ff' a = 'y'>
TIP. Wield a weapon of a higher grade than your suitable weapon and you earn up to 120% Experience, and at night you earn 150% Experience on top of that. Add a party on top of both and you earn the most Experience possible.
<<#color ='ffffff' a = 'y'>

3) Rank system
<<#color ='ffffff' a = 'y'>

The vampire Masters decided to grant ranks to their loyal servants to honor their deeds on the battlefield. They also proclaimed that vampires who reached high ranks would be taught special magic. This was also a way to establish a firm chain of command and raise morale for the great war to come. With it, the vampire warriors strengthened their loyalty to their Masters and at the same time gained a more organized fighting force.

Rank marks by stage (each mark from left to right is 1 level higher, 5 levels per stage - e.g. the leftmost Sergeant mark is level 6)

o Rank Experience
<<#color ='ffffff' a = 'y'>

- You rise in Rank by accumulating Rank Experience. Slayers earn Rank Experience by hunting monsters and fighting Vampires. When it reaches the Rank Experience goal, you are promoted. Once your Rank reaches a certain level, you can learn new rank skills.
- When you or a Slayer in your party kills an enemy, the Rank Experience grows with the size of the party and is shared automatically according to the members' levels. This bonus is only shared with members within 8 tiles of the leader.

		#SLAYERPAGE
		[==Title==]
		   Fraud investigation and recovery notice
		[==Sender==]
		   3
		[==Level Table==]
		   -1 -1 -1 -1 -1 -1
		[==Detail==]
* Period: September 15, 2004 - December 15, 2004 (3 months)
* Types of fraud we can investigate and recover
  - Trade fraud (lying about the amount in a trade)
  - Impersonation fraud (deceiving with a character with a similar name)
  - Storage character fraud
      (posing as the item storage character of someone you know well)
* Screenshot evidence for each type of fraud
< file = 'HelpMessage_92' pos = 'C'>  

  - Trade fraud : a screenshot of the agreed price, a screenshot of the price shown in the exchange window
< file = 'HelpMessage_93' pos = 'C'>


  - Impersonation fraud : a screenshot of the conversation where they lied about who they are,
                     a screenshot of the item in the exchange window

< file = 'HelpMessage_94' pos = 'C'>


  - Storage character fraud : a screenshot of the conversation where they lied about who they are, a screenshot of the item in the exchange window
* Recovery and seizure policy
  - Handled under the hacking recovery policy
  - Report during this period and get your items recovered to receive Gilles de Rais Beads
  - Accounts that attempted fraud are permanently seized on all servers
* How to report
  - Report within 3 days of being defrauded
  - Attach the relevant screenshots to your report
  - Be sure to include your server, ID and character name (your own) in the report
  - Report through the DKx Online homepage (dkxonline.com.cn)
{End}
