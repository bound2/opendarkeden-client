The ultimate healing technique of Elquines, the Spirit King of water, with the power to awaken those near death. Unlike other races' resurrection skills, it can also save those near death from a distance.

Revives a dying comrade. A revived Ousters comes back with 10% of total HP; to revive from a distance or raise the HP restored on revival, you must learn Soul Rebirth Mastery. Mastering the skill grants a substantial bonus.
