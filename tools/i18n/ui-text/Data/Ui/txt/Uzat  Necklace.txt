Horus, the god of light in Egyptian mythology, is the son of Osiris, god of the underworld, and the goddess Isis. He took revenge on his uncle Set, who had murdered his father Osiris, and is said to have lost one eye in the fight. The amulet that renders this eye of Horus as a hieroglyph is the Uzat. The Uzat is an amulet for health and safety, and the dead buried with it are said to be reborn in the afterlife and rise to the heavens.
