With their innate magical nature, vampires can easily take the form of other animals. The fast and powerful wolf is one of the animals vampires turn into most often. As a wolf your movement speed rises by 150%, so this spell is mainly used for traveling long distances. In wolf form you can also attack, and as a bonus, eating a monster's corpse restores a little HP. However, your Defense is lower than in vampire form, so this transformation is ill suited for use in combat.
