The burgonet is a helmet developed for cavalry in the early 16th century. Its skull is ball-shaped with a crest like a cockscomb, but it also has a peak like a baseball cap and a bevor. These are joined together, riveted on both sides of the skull, but can be opened upward by releasing the hooks on both sides. Today the old burgonet has been extended into a helmet that protects the whole face, fitted with sights, communications gear and the like.
