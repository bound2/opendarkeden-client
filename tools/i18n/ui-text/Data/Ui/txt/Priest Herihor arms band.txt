Opal as a gem means the noble opal, which carries the meanings of loyalty, religious zeal, prayer and assurance. In the Middle Ages opal was called the 'eye stone' (ophtalmius), said to cure eye diseases and to make its owner invisible. The stone set in the crown of the Holy Roman Empire is also said to have shone at night. Praised as a sacred gem from then on, opal came to be loved by many as a symbol of protection from all harm.
