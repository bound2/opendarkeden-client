A relic found in China, a blade with a striking grip made of human bone. King Wu of Zhou is said to have given it to General Heoje, who had earned the greatest merit when the tyrant Zhou of Yin was driven out. It was then handed down to the bravest generals until it was lost during the Sui dynasty. Its blade is so heavy that an ordinary person can barely lift it, and its edge is rather blunt. As a Chinese blade, it is the type that cleaves the body with brute force from above.
