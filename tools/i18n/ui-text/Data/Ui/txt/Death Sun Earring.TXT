An earring designed to strengthen a Vampire's resistance to the sun. However resistant they may be, it is true that Vampires grow somewhat weaker under a blazing sun. The Death Sun Earring, however, can absorb all the sunlight falling within a certain radius and reflect it back into the sky or in a chosen direction. It is made of silver, with a red ruby set in the center of a sun-patterned medallion.
