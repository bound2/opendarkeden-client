Holy water, the product of Create Holy Water, the first skill of the clergy's Enchant domain. The amount made depends on the water bottle; this one is made with a small water bottle. Holy water means "sacred water": clean water specially blessed by a priest. Used for religious purification, it signifies washing away all that is unclean by driving out the sin and demons that hinder holiness. When the faithful dip their fingertips in holy water and make the sign of the cross on entering a church, they do so to recall the promise made at their baptism and in the hope that body and soul are cleansed as they enter.
