The most basic early submachine gun model, favored mainly by special forces and counter-terrorist units across Europe. Its firepower is lower than a rifle's, but it is light and easy to carry. The MD-4 is mostly used in hostage rescue operations, reportedly because an AR has so much firepower that it could hurt the hostages. It is not suited to killing Vampires, but SMGs modified for anti-vampire use, such as the MD-4a, are widely used.
