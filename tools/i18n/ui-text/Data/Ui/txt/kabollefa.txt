A ring modeled on the Kabollefa, a kind of oncidium, a holy flower that grows in the tropics. This plant, whose flower language is the blazing tropical sun and a pure heart, is one of the holy objects the Holy See uses in its rites for exorcising Vampires. A handmade silver ring modeled on the holy Kabollefa, with intricate patterns and made only with the Holy See's permission, it is a rare accessory that is hard to find on the market.
