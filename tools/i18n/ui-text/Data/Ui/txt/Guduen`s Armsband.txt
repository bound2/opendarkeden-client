Guduen, a kind of water spirit that once lived in Lake Timor, was a beautiful golden-haired woman who sometimes played on the lake in a small boat. She had many chances to meet humans, and stories of this spirit marrying a human man are commonly told throughout the Timor region. But striking them is forbidden: strike one three times and she returns to the lake. When she returns to the lake, she is said to leave her ornaments behind.
