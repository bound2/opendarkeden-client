The Sirens lived on a small island near Sicily and are said to have lured sailors to their doom by singing in lovely voices. When the Argonauts led by Jason came near the island, Orpheus played his lyre with all his might to counter their song and kept the expedition safe.
Later, when Odysseus's ship passed by on the way home from the Trojan War, he followed Circe's advice, stopped his men's ears with wax and had himself bound to the mast, so that he alone heard their song and still passed by unharmed.
After that, the Sirens regretted being beaten by humans and are said to have thrown themselves into the sea.
