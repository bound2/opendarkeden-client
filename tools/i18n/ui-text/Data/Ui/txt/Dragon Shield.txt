A shield with a dragon emblem embossed on it. The center of the shield is decorated with wood, but the back and rim are worked in sheet metal, giving it great durability and shock absorption. The Dragon Shield is made of steel, yet it is not heavy for a steel shield, reportedly because it is made of a specially produced alloy steel. What sets it apart is that it is said to have been handed down in the family of Vlad Tepes, one of the Vampire Masters. He carried it into war before he became a vampire. Using this shield does not, of course, turn you into a vampire. Yet no one knows how it came to be here. Perhaps a Slayer who infiltrated Vlad's castle brought it back as a trophy.
