A cross representing the hill of Calvary, whose name comes from the Aramaic Golgotha. It is said to have been made by several popes in the Vatican to commemorate Christ's death on the cross under Pontius Pilate. It is modeled on the cross Christ bore and the crown of thorns he wore. The clergy of Jerusalem are said never to cease raising this cross high to the sky and climbing the hill along the path where Christ bore his cross. By using it, the clergy are said to experience for themselves the suffering of the persecuted Christ.
