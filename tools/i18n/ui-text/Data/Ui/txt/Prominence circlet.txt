A circlet that visualizes fire, the most important element to spirit mages and the embodiment of change itself. The symbol above comes in particular from an illustration in a 17th-century book by spirit mages. So central was fire that the magic of fire spirit mages was said to 'begin in fire and end in fire'; fire was the most important means of transmuting matter, and since it transmutes imperfect matter into unified, stable matter, it is also a symbol of perfection and unity. Not only for fire but for all spirit magic, it helps the wearer cast with stability and perfection.
