A wristlet holding the extract of wormwood, a plant witches often also call 'henbane'. Wormwood is another plant witches used to make their ointments. It has been used in magic since ancient times; the Assyrians already used it to enter a trance and prophesy. Wormwood is a poisonous herb containing narcotic substances; it not only numbs the senses but can kill in an overdose. Though the plant carries many dangers, magic tools made with its extract are said to be able to foresee crises to come.
