Cloth armor is armor made of fabric; since its forms varied so much through the ages, the name covers all armor made of cloth. It is quilted with cotton padding and remained popular for a long time regardless of the era. This type of armor was not especially effective, but it did a good enough job of softening the impact of blunt weapons and the like. Combining this aketon with a coat has given rebirth to an effective Coat that is light, allows great freedom of movement and blunts the force of blows.
