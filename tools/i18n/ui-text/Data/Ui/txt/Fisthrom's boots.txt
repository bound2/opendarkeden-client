Fisthrom is the spirit of storms, a fiercely aggressive spirit that controls the air to raise terribly destructive winds. It is said that only spirit mages recognized by Minerva can command him. These few high spirit mages use their power over Fisthrom to imbue items such as shoes with his energy. Items that receive Fisthrom's energy grant great speed, but ordinary folk find them hard to control, and even intermediate spirit mages can only barely use them.
