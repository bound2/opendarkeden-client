An unusual bracelet for Slayers. It is set with five gemstones and is distinguished by a holy Catholic mark inscribed on its inner side. Behind the five stones are drawn five marks: the cross, sign of the Lord's army; the wings of the heavenly host; the heavenly shield; Pegasus, the winged horse; and the sword of the Archangel Michael. Legend says they give their wearer the power of the Holy Spirit.
