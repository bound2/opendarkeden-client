The bewitching allure of Vampires is already recognized as one of their most powerful weapons. The charm of female Vampires in particular can break down even an enemy's guard. The newly born young Vampires wanted beautiful garments that would make the most of it. Because thin-cut leather is stitched in many layers, it also effectively stops attacks from silver bullets and swords. The Masters, however, look down on this garment as vulgar, so those who seek a Master's favor do not choose it lightly.
