The Sword, one of the oldest weapons humanity has used, is the word for a double-edged blade. Used mainly to thrust at and cut enemies, the sword was beloved by warriors for thousands of years, but from the late 16th century, when guns and cannon using gunpowder came into widespread use, it steadily lost its usefulness, and in modern times it was hardly used except for decoration and ceremony.
However, once it became known that ordinary guns could do Vampires no harm beyond a temporary shock, Slayers abandoned their guns and began to master the art of cutting and stabbing Vampires in a single stroke. And to gain the upper hand in the fight against Vampires, they set out in search of the great swords renowned in history.
Swords are lighter and faster than blades, and you can also equip a shield to raise your defense. However, they have the drawback of dealing less damage per hit than blades.
