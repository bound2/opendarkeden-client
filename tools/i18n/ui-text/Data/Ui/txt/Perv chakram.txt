The Perv Chakram is said to have been used by the Sikhs of ancient northern India. What is unusual about this weapon is that it is a throwing weapon that 'cuts'; spinning in flight, it has a range of 40-50 m. In tests it reportedly severed a bamboo stalk 3 cm thick at 30 m. But ordinary humans today lack the strength and skill to use it, so the chakram became an almost forgotten weapon, surviving only in museums and documents. Sioram, who was researching many aspects of Ousters training, thought the chakram he had seen while traveling in India would be ideal for Ousters to train their bodily strength and skill. With his knowledge and craftsmanship he recreated the Perv Chakram the Sikhs had used, and just as he expected, the chakram became the perfect weapon for the Ousters.
