A Vampire ring carved from human bone. It was found in an antique shop in Thailand, and its patterns and craftsmanship show a style unknown until now. All that is known is that radiocarbon dating suggests the bone dates from before the 2nd century BC. When worn, its band tightens by itself, and not a single Vampire who wore it is said to have survived. A mysterious ring from which one is freed only by one's own destruction.
