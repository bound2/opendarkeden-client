The Tournament Cuisse is the lower-body part of the Tournament Armor, the armor worn, as the name says, in the tournaments where Western European knights tested their skill. To defend against blows it had to be quite thick, so it was naturally heavy and somewhat impractical. With advanced modern technology, however, it has been remade in a light aluminum alloy that keeps the grandeur of Tournament Armor while being light and giving solid protection, and it is said to be a favorite of many warriors.
