The cross in the middle stands for 'the Lord', and the small beads set beside it stand for 'the Holy Mother'. A rosary ring is used when praying the Rosary; rosario is Latin for "garland of roses", and the devotion was handed down in the Western Church. It is the most widely practiced prayer; one account holds that the Holy Mother appeared to Saint Dominic and taught him how to pray it, another that when early believers prayed by reading the 150 Psalms, those who could not read joined in by saying the Lord's Prayer and the Hail Mary 150 times.
