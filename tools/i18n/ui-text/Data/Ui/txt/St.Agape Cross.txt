The cross of St. Helena. Around 325 she built a church on Calvary, the hill where Christ was nailed to the cross and died, and there she found the cross on which Christ had hung. From then on she came to stand for the cross. To honor St. Helena's achievements, it was made in the 14th century by a Benedictine order of women for nuns who wished to honor and follow her beautiful heart and charity, and religious who have received Christ's revelation are said to use this cross in memory of her heart.
