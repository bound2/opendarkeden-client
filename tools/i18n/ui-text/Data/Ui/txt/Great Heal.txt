The priests, who had come to feel the limits of healing prayers that drew on the Holy Spirit, urgently needed more effective and powerful healing magic for an ever fiercer war. Hearing of this, high-ranking priests of the Vatican studied Great Heal, a strong healing prayer handed down since the Middle Ages, and succeeded in making it even more effective. Great Heal has proven not only to heal powerfully but also to cure Blood Drain by vampires of Master rank and above.
