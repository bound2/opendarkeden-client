Evoking a demon of purgatory, the Dark Screamer is a vampire that delights in draining the blood of living creatures. Addicted to blood, it drains everything it sees, and every creature that enters its sight must accept the misfortune of becoming its prey.

Ancient documents about vampires depict them draining everything in their path like blood-drinking ghouls, and they are also called 'the Demons Who Revel in the Feast of Blood'.
