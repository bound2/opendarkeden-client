Unlike the double-edged sword, the Blade has one highly developed edge and was used mainly for cutting. Blades of superb quality are still made in the Far East, in China, Japan and Korea, and a wide variety of blades were also made in the Middle East and Europe.
Blades are heavier and slower than swords, but powerful enough to cut a Vampire in two with a single stroke. Almost all the blades in DarkEden must be used with both hands, so they have the drawback that you cannot equip a shield with them. They are favored mainly by attack-minded warriors.
