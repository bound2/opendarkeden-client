The original word means 'lion's tail'. As its name comes from the lion, it was also used as a symbol of royalty. The scimitar is the representative sword of Persia, and it is also known as the sword from which the saber originated.
Its distinctive features are a body curved as gracefully as a crescent moon and a hilt that curves the opposite way from the blade. The more curved the blade, the more powerful its cuts. Early scimitars had straight blades, but since the swordsmanship common in Persia was about cutting enemies down with downward strokes, the sword took on a supple curve to suit that purpose.
