A male Vampire coat made by building on the strengths of the coat recognized as the finest among Vampires. A privilege of the mightiest Vampires, it is said to hold something that raises the wearer's magical power to its peak. Depending on the magic cast on it, the coat can be given different magical abilities. The coat's own magic is so strong that a young Vampire who wears it may be destroyed. Its strengths are that, though the material feels substantial, it is light and easy to move in, and it gives high full-body protection in battle against Slayers.
