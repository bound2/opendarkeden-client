- Vampire legends of the West Indies
On Grenada, one of the islands of the West Indies, there is a vampire called the Loogaroo. The Loogaroo is an old woman who has made a pact with the Devil, and in return for being taught magic she serves him human blood by night. Every night the Loogaroo goes to a place where a certain mysterious tree grows, peels off her skin and hides it inside the tree, then flies about in every direction as a glowing ball of fire. It is said that scattering a mixture of sand and rice in front of the gate before sunset and locking the door tight will keep the Loogaroo away.
- Vampire legends of Polynesia
In the middle of the Pacific, where the islands of Polynesia lie scattered, there is a vampire called the Tii. It is not a dead person brought back to life but a living person who, in order to torment an enemy, has secretly eaten the flesh and blood of the dead and so become a vampire. By eating a dead person's flesh and blood one can speak with that person's spirit, and the spirit then takes revenge on one's behalf. However, if such a person is exposed to the smoke of a certain burning herb, he will confess that he has become a Tii.
