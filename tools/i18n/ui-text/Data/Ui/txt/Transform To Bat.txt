With their innate magical nature, vampires can easily take the form of other animals. The small, swift bat in particular is often used by vampires to flee when in danger. As a bat your movement speed rises by 200%, and you take no damage from ordinary melee weapons, only from guns and missile spells. However, in bat form you cannot attack or use Blood Drain, and your Defense drops sharply, so one mistake can get you killed even more easily.
