A switch-type mine that goes off when the detonator fitted on top is stepped on; simple to operate and easy to carry, it is widely used. Flat and cylindrical, it is buried underground and blasts straight upward. Being the easiest mine to hide, it is used everywhere, but it is said that people often lose track of where they buried it and set it off themselves. It was often used as an anti-tank mine and was made to explode when enemy troops passed close by.
