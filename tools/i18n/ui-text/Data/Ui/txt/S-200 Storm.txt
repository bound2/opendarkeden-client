The Mammoth99 brought back the mighty power of the shotgun, but ordinary Slayers were reluctant to carry it because it was hard to carry. So the S-200 was made to combine strong firepower with a focus on lightness. Its barrel was changed to a cutting-edge composite material and it is fitted with AKS (Anti Kick System), which minimizes recoil, making it the most trusted of all SG models developed so far. A product of Smith, maker of the GOOSE, its firepower is strong enough to show you a Vampire's guts in one shot. Its nickname is "Storm". However, its high price puts it out of reach of ordinary Slayers.
