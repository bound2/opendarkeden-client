Dryads live in trees and share their trees' fate. Outwardly they are beauties with small bodies and green hair, typical nymphs of Greek myth, but within their own domain they hold considerable power, which makes them far more dangerous than they look. The only way to capture a dryad is to bind her with a shoot of the very tree she lives in. A dryad bound this way comes to dwell in the shoot, and her power can then be used.
