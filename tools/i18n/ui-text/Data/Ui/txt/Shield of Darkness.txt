No one knows who crafted it. It has the shape of a simple square shield and bears no gems, but its red copper shines so brightly that it looks closer to gold than copper, and rumor has it that this is because the gold is mixed with red blood. Whatever the truth, thanks to the craftsman's meticulous care it holds a blessing of blood that protects the Vampire who wears this earring, greatly reducing the wounds taken in battle.
