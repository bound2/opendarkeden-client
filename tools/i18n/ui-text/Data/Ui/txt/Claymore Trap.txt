It looks just like a square lunch box bent into a half-moon curve. It is an olive-drab plastic case packed with high explosive, its top face lined with some 700 steel balls the size of peas. When the charge explodes, these balls fly forward in a 60-degree arc. It is connected by a long electric wire (30 m) to a firing device at the end, and pressing the device generates a current that sets it off. Its power is tremendous.
