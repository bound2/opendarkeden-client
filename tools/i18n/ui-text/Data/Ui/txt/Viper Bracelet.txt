A Vampire bracelet engraved with snakes: two snakes open their mouths wide to hold up a sapphire in the middle. Here the snake means the 'tempter' as portrayed in the Bible. Revelation 12:9 says "that old serpent, called the Devil, and Satan...", and it is said to symbolize Satan and evil. The snakes on this bracelet stand for wisdom and the power of darkness, and legend has it that the bracelet grants both powers to its wearer.
