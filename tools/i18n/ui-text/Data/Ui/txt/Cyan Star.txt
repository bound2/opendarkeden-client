Surely no animal matches the agility of a bird flying through the blue sky. The eagle is such a bird: it soars leisurely through the sky, and once it picks a target it swoops at great speed to seize its prey.

The Cyan Star is an event item that can be exchanged for items or money with the NPCs below, depending on your race and location.

 Field Headquarters NPC : Kaiser
 Warrior Guild NPC : Conan
 Soldier Guild NPC : Smith
 Priest Guild NPC : Stoyanov
 Vampire Village NPC : Vrykolakas

Use: Stars can be exchanged once you have 20 / 40 / 60 / 80 / 100 of them, for high-grade items with an Attack Speed option or for money.

* To exchange this item for money, you must first collect stars of the same kind, then go to Kaiser, Conan, Smith, Stoyanov, Vrykolakas or the like, select 'I want to exchange my stars for money' and left-click to make the exchange.
