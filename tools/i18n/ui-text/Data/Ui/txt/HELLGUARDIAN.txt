The Hell Guardian is a cruel vampire who hacks her victims apart with a scythe as tall as she is. Her blows are so devastating that it is hard to believe she is a female vampire; countless people have been cut in two by her scythe, and not only Slayers but many vampires too are said to have fallen victim to her.

Almost nothing is known about the scythe she wields, save that it is called 'the Scythe That Drinks Blood'.
