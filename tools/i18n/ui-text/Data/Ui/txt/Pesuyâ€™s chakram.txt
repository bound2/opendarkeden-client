A chakram modeled on the shape of a bindrune that brings wealth, made by combining runes, the magical script of the ancient Europeans. Runes are an alphabet the peoples of Northern Europe used from the 1st century AD to the Middle Ages. They were a mysterious script used not only for writing but often for magic and divination as well. Each rune carries a magical meaning of its own, but to heighten the magical effect several runes were sometimes bound together into a new sign, called a bindrune. Bindrunes were used to fulfill the small wishes people always have.
