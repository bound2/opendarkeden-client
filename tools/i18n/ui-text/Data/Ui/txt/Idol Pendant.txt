A necklace bearing an ornament shaped like a Vampire idol. Vampires made these to honor their idols; of those thought to be the earliest, the surviving ones are over 200 years old. The pendants differ in shape according to their idol, and so do the ideals they pursue. The power passed to the wearer depends on who the idol is, and the older the pendant, the more power it has stored up. The strongest of pendants, with the best defense and durability of any before it, it is said that only the strong can use it.
