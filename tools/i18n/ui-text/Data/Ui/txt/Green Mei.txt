Those who grieved over Lavinia's sacrifice kept up their research into making a Meigu, a doll to house the soul of Carissas. Along the way they managed to complete a small wooden doll that could hold not the soul itself, but at least its spiritual energy. Though a failure, it was meaningful enough as a by-product of the work toward the Meigu. The Ousters then began to use this Mei as a vessel for storing soul energy, the source of their magic.
