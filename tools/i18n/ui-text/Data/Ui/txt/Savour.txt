A sword made as long as possible while light enough for a cavalryman to wield one-handed on horseback. Length 70-120 cm, weight 1.7-2.4 kg, width 2-4 cm. The most common type took a middle path between the straight thrusting type and the curved slashing type, with only the tip rounded.
The tips also differ slightly by use: thrusting swords have a spear-shaped tip, slashing swords a hatchet-shaped tip, and those meant for both a false-edge tip (False Edge: only the end of the sword is double-edged, and that part is at least 1/3 of the blade's total length).
