The BFG-50 is a modified carbine, developed into a sniper rifle by adding a scope. It is relatively light for a sniper rifle and makes up for the poor accuracy that is the weakness of the carbine form. Easy to carry and simple in appearance, it seemed to fulfill the long-held wish of many snipers. However, production problems limited it to a small run, and it is said to trade among enthusiasts at prices hard even to imagine.
