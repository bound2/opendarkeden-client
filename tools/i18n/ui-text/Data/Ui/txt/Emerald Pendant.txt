A necklace for Slayers with a black emerald ornament, discoloured to a dark sheen. Because it looks like an eagle's eye, this pendant is also called the 'Eagle Eye'. Wearing it raises accuracy against a target, and some say it grants foresight, the power of the emerald itself. When it locks onto a target it is said to change shape as if an eye were opening, raising accuracy. The emerald signifies immortality, hope, youth, spring and the waxing moon.
