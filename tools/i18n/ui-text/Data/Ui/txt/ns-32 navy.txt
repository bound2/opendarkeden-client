A model used in Vietnam by the U.S. NAVY SEALs, classed as a basic submachine gun. Mounted on a tripod or bipod, it can also serve as a light or heavy machine gun. The Navy began as the T44 model, which adopted the belt feed of the German M42 machine gun and the operating system of the FG42 machine gun. It thus developed into a model that basically combined the two systems of the M42 and FG 42. The Navy features quick barrel changes, light weight, simplicity, and the ability to work as both a heavy and a light machine gun. It was later improved in many ways, but structural flaws left it overshadowed by the popularity of the Belgian masterpiece, the FN-MAG machine gun, so it never spread very widely.
