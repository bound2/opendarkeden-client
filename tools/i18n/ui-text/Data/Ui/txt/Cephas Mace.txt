Cephas, meaning 'rock' in Aramaic, is the name Christ gave to Peter, one of his 12 disciples, signifying the rock of the Church that would spread across the world. This cross of the rock was first made by monks of the Dominican order within the Augustinians to spread the grace of Christ throughout the world. In the hope that Christ's grace would spread far and wide, it was made exactly a thousand years after Christ's resurrection.
