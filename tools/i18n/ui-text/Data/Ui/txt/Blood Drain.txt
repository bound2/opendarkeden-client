Through Blood Drain a vampire enjoys an imperfect eternal life. A vampire who goes too long without drinking blood slowly dies, like a human starved for days. Yet a vampire can survive far longer without blood than a human can without food, simply by sleeping soundly in a comfortable coffin.
The special power held in blood is the true force that moves a vampire's dead body. A vampire can spend the blood it has drunk to work special magic. This ability is granted to every vampire who has reason and is aware of its own vampiric nature.
