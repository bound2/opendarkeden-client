VERUS stands for Vampire Erasing Revolution Unit System. It is a bullpup design (a rifle with the magazine and chamber behind the trigger) that fires 5.56mm-sVK rounds. It is lighter than the VK rifles, and its trademarks are a large carrying handle running the length of the rifle and near-perfect left-right symmetry, so it can be operated with either hand. Its barrel is long for its short overall length, it takes a telescopic sight, and it works without major malfunctions even in harsh terrain and weather. It is a gun that shows Smith's reliability everywhere.
