A bracelet for Vampires. The blue-violet iolite in its center resembles a blue sapphire when polished and has long been called the water sapphire. It usually shows several colors, but looks colorless when viewed side-on. This two-faced stone also stands for the sea, symbol of the cycle of death and life. The bracelet is said to have been made by a primordial Vampire who longed for a finite life, free of the constraints of light.
