Where other SMG models lost other features by modifying existing SMGs to boost one particular capability, the EasyRider is regarded as one of the guns most faithful to the basics of the machine gun. It adopts a detachable stock for easier carrying, and its barrel is weighted to reduce recoil. It doubles the power of earlier SMGs with a sight incorporating ATS (Advanced Targeting System) for higher accuracy and state-of-the-art rifling for a dramatic boost in firepower. Switching between automatic and semi-automatic needs no separate selector: pull the trigger halfway for semi-automatic and all the way for automatic.
