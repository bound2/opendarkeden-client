The FURY, the ambitious rifle of I&S (famous for the "ZIG"), is a bullpup rifle whose entire body is plastic, as are even its firing parts such as the trigger, sear and hammer. It comes standard with a 1.5x scope that doubles as a carrying handle, and it is accurate enough to snipe a football-sized target 300 meters away. It uses 5.56mm VK magazines, and the Fury's bolt and ejection port can be switched to either side for left- or right-handed shooters. The Fury shows a superb aesthetic sense in design and concept, and offered a brilliant answer to the size and weight problems of the assault rifle.
