A book in which Christ's followers set down, through history, the works and words of Christ and their own confessions and testimony. The Bible is 'the Word of God', God's revelation and testimony. The Bible, the scripture of Christianity, is divided into 39 books of the Old Testament and 27 of the New. 'Old' refers to before Christ and 'New' to after Christ, while 'Testament' means God's covenant of salvation with humanity. It is made up of history, poetry, prophecy, epistles and more, and the surviving text was copied onto paper from what had been written on parchment until the 15th century.
