At the Field Headquarters you can learn Heal and Enchant skills from Tanya.
At the Field Headquarters you can learn gun skills from Jacques.
At the Field Headquarters you can learn sword and blade skills from Kaiser.
Press Control+H to view the help.
Press Control+K to see your Domain levels and skill levels.
The hotkey that opens the belt's quick slots is Control+~.
Which stats rise depends on your weapon.
With blades & swords, STR rises more than your other stats.
With guns, DEX rises more than your other stats.
With crosses & maces, INT rises more than your other stats.
Sum usually means the total of STR + DEX + INT.
Keep using a sword as your weapon and your Sword Domain level rises.
Keep using a blade as your weapon and your Blade Domain level rises.
Keep using a gun as your weapon and your Gun Domain level rises.
Keep using a cross as your weapon and your Heal Domain level rises.
Keep using a mace as your weapon and your Enchant Domain level rises.
To recover HP, right-click an ampul.
A dark red stain on your HP bar means your blood has been drained.
Monsters and Vampires can only drain your blood when your HP bar is at 30% or below.
You raise a skill's level by using that skill again and again.
To use a skill, bring it up in the skill window and right-click the target.
Skills are learned as your Domain levels rise.
As a skill's level rises, you get a small Damage and To Hit bonus.
Skill level shows how well you have mastered a skill, and it rises as you use the skill.
Your Domain levels rise according to the weapon you use.
You can learn skills once a Domain reaches level 5.
Soldiers load bullets by right-clicking a magazine.
Soldiers using guns: each gun takes a different magazine, so buy the right one.
Soldiers using guns: the number next to the bullet icon at the top left of the screen shows how many rounds are left.
Put ampuls or magazines on your belt to use them easily with the hotkeys (F1~F8).
The radio is an item for traveling to other areas.
You can buy a radio from the NPC who sells motorcycles.
You can only use a radio once your stats total more than 80.
Motorcycles can be bought from the NPC in front of each guild building.
Motorcycles have no durability, so they never need repairs.
After you reconnect, your motorcycle disappears from where it was.
If your Alignment is bad, you can raise it by donating money to an NPC.
Slayers can set their respawn point only once their stat total (sum) is 40 or more.
Use the Helicopter on the roof of a guild building to travel to another guild building.
You can buy equipment on the 2nd and 3rd floors of the guild buildings.
You can set a respawn point at each guild so you come back to life wherever suits you.
On the 3rd floor of each guild building, you can donate money to raise your Alignment up to Neutral (-2500).
Silver plating adds a sacred power called holy damage to your base Damage.
If you want to play as a Vampire, have your blood drained and then go without treatment for 12 hours in real time.
If you are not treated after having your blood drained, you turn into a Vampire after 12 hours, whether or not you are logged in.
When you turn into a Vampire, your Slayer stats and items are lost.
When you are wounded or your blood has been drained, go to 'Dennis' for treatment.
Use the ampuls in your inventory window by right-clicking them.
To make holy water, buy a water bottle, select the skill and use it on the water bottle.
At character creation, your starting stats total 30.
Slayers grow through a skill-up system, not a level-up system.

Resistance (RESISTANCE) : The power to withstand Vampire POISON, ACID, CURSE and BLOOD magic.
Resistance (RESISTANCE) : Increased only by items and certain skills.
The total of your Domain levels is capped at 100.
If your Domain levels total 100 or more, the highest-level Domain other than the one that just rose goes down.
