Also called Adam's Tears, it has a bluish crystal color.
Legend says that long ago Lilith, mother of all Vampires and earth mother goddess, was murdered by the 12 Elders after being falsely accused by Dew. This ended the age in which the Vampire race had steadily prospered around Lilith, and the race slowly fell into decline. Then Adam, holding his wife Lilith's body to his chest, cried out and wept, and it is said that his tears hardened into the Blue Drop. Also known as Adam's Tears, the Blue Drop is found in tiny quantities only in parts of eastern Eslania, and its value is said to exceed that of diamonds. Even in nature it occurs only as small crystals, which seems to be why it bears the name of tears. It is also said to help anyone of special power who obtains it draw out that power, but no one has yet confirmed this.
