Designed to fix the problems of earlier helmets, it was built with a rethink of the helmet's weight and of its infrared system. It uses a precise object recognition system that makes up in particular for the earlier difficulty in recognizing Vampires, which give off no body heat. Its dynamic shock-dispersing design and precise pinpointing of objects earned it high satisfaction among Slayers. Its drawback is that infrared scanning is done manually. Fitted with ISS (Infrared Scanning System).
