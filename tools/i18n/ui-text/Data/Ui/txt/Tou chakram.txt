A Chakram is a weapon made of a flat metal ring with a blade all around its outer edge. The ring is about 2-4 cm wide and 10-30 cm in diameter, and it weighs about 0.15-0.5 kg. It is used either by putting the index finger through the ring and spinning it to build speed before throwing, or by gripping it between thumb and index finger and throwing it like a discus. However, it seems no throwing method has been handed down correctly. The Tou Chakram can be called the most basic form of the Chakram.
