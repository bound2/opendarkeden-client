A Chakram shaped after a symbol of the Haisa Indians that awakens new hope. The symbol comes from the tattoos of the Haisa Indians of North America and depicts the 'moon', which the Haisa regarded as the source of life. Unlike the sun, the moon hides itself for three days around the new moon as though dead, then shows itself again. So the Haisa saw the moon as the source of life that gives birth to new life. The moon symbol carries the Haisa Indians' hope that even after the death of despair, new birth and a happy life will come.
