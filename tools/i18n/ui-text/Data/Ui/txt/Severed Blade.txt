This blade seems born to cut down many foes. The Severed Blade's most distinctive feature is its form, as if the tip had simply been lopped off blunt, widening from the hilt toward the tip.
This blade stirred controversy among swordsman Slayers who valued technique alone, and was even called "a thug's weapon for brutes with strength to spare", but in fact it was made and used by a Slayer hailed as a "master swordsman" to develop a sword style of his own. With its center of gravity at the tip, every swing carries power and speed, and it has sent the heads of countless Vampires flying.
