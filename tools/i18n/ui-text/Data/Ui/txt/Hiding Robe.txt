A Vampire coat that is a reinforced version of the men's Vampire coat made of special vinyl. Building on that coat's strength of resisting damage even under fairly high external pressure, it was made so as not to deform under most pressure. Vampires who have trained in magic to suit their own tastes often imbue their coats with magical abilities to raise their power to the full. This coat was made to improve the magic power of young Vampires.
