Shrouded head to toe in bandages and robes, the Reaper looks like a gloomy monk. It creeps up silently, throws groups of Slayers into chaos and drives them to their deaths, which is why it is also called 'the Grim Reaper'.

They creep up silently and throw many Slayers into a panic, and trying to avoid them often leads to disaster. Slayers who see a Reaper's gleaming eyes are said to feel a fear that turns their knees to water.
