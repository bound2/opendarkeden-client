The spangenhelm, the helmet of the Franks, appeared around the 6th century, and at the time only people of a certain rank could wear it. In the modern war against Vampires, however, the helm's structure proved well suited to all kinds of modifications, and it was reborn as the 'Spangen Helmet' by fitting lenses with built-in 'infrared sensor' and 'telescope' functions.
