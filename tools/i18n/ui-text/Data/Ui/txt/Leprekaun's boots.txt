Leprekaun, the spirit of chaos, can throw those who receive his power into a state of chaos much like a berserker's, but the human body then moves only as Leprekaun directs. He also has the power to disturb the flow of a human's mana so that magic cannot be used. For the Ousters, however, bearing Leprekaun's power merely raises their attributes. That is why items imbued with this spirit's power are widely used.
