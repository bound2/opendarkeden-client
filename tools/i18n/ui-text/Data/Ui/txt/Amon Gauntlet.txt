A gauntlet made from the claws of Amon, one of the 72 demons of the Lemegeton. Amon is a demon of hell with the head of an owl, the body of a wolf and the tail of a serpent; originally the Egyptian god Amun, he became a demon through medieval demonology. Gauntlets made from his mighty wolf's claws have long been famed as rare trophies that only the strong could own, and it is said that very few people have one.
