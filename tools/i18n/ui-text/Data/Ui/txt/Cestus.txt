A glove shaped from tough cowhide into the form of a hand, reinforced with worked iron plate, and finished with studs on the back of the hand so that it can both defend and attack. Care was also taken to protect the wrist, so its overall defense is very high. Being made of leather and plate, its durability is somewhat poor, but it is a very popular glove because it can both attack and defend. A useful gauntlet in unarmed close combat, it hits the opponent hard.
