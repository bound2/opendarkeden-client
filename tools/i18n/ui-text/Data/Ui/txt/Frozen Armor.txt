Spreads an invisible wall of ice over yourself or an ally, reducing part of the damage from melee attacks. A target slowed by this skill is slowed in everything, including Transform To Bat, the Ousters' wind spirit, Transform To Wolf, summoning a Motorcycle and casting Fast Move-type skills. Only attack speed and casting speed are unaffected. The reduction and duration depend on INT and skill level. Mastering the skill grants a substantial bonus.
