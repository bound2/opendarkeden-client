The Sprite, called the spirit of invisibility, was once a popular summon among summoners and is said to have made the summoner invisible. It has no particular form and exists through the bond between spirit master and spirit, and it can be found through ancient magic. Today, because the ancient spellbooks are damaged, only feeble spells remain, enough to breathe part of its power into shoes, armor and the like. Which of its powers such items reveal has yet to be confirmed.
