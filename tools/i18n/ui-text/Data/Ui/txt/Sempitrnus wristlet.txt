A wristlet of harmony and peaceful balance, bearing a design of three small waves forming one shape, encircled in turn by a larger wave. Three elements forming one shape is called a triad. Symbols of this form are found all over the world; by one interpretation, the three waves stand for past, present and future, and the wave around them for Eternity. Through eternity, the ultimate time, past, present and future are united. This interpretation cannot be called entirely right or wrong, but the triad is certainly a symbol of three elements joining as one to form a universal whole.
