A mace supplied directly by the Holy See in Rome; it is not the cleric's own property. Through this mace a cleric can connect directly with the source of his faith.
Should any cleric become drunk on its power and let pride or unbelief take root in his heart, he will become a slave of this mace, and it will be nothing more than an ordinary club. Any Vampire who sees this mace in Eslania is already no longer a Vampire.
