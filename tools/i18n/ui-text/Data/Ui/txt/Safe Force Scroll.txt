While an underground tunnel was being dug to build an EVE supply base in the north of Mount Rodin, a rock wall collapsed in a disaster. During the rescue work, a large number of scrolls written in an ancient language were found together with thousands of corpses.
Thought to date from the late 14th century, this scroll was reported by the black magic researcher Yobel to hold special magical power. It is said to affect every living creature in exactly the same way.
