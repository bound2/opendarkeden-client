A cross the Pope bestowed on monarchs to secure their loyalty as Protestant power grew in the 16th century. Its surface is lavishly gilded, and the Protestants harshly condemned it as a symbol of the corrupt old Church. Until the 19th century it was treated as a mere symbol, but a scholar of religion revealed that it holds holy power. The cross is said to have been made during the reign of Pope Clement IX in the 16th century.
