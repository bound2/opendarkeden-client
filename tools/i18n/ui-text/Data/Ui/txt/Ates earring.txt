A beautiful earring found with many other treasures in an underground passage of an old castle in southern France in the 17th century. It is set with 12 diamonds packed in two rows, and it is said that most who came to own it died within 10 years. Because of this it drifted across Europe for 300 years, changing owners more than 50 times. Carrying a curse no human could endure, it finally fell into the hands of Vampires.
