A shield made to cover the whole body, built from tortoiseshell following a huge whirlwind pattern with the emphasis on light weight. It is large and awkward to carry, but light and very durable. Because of that focus on lightness, its drawback as armor is that the tortoiseshell does not absorb much impact. Because of the whirlwind pattern on its surface, this shield is also called the 'Spiral shield'.
