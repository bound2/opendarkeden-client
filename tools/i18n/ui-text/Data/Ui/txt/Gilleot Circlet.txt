Gilleot was once a higher spirit in charge of emotion. But he always envied the Spirit King's power and position and never missed a chance to speak ill of the Spirit King to the other spirits. In the end his slander reached the Spirit King's ears, and the enraged Spirit King locked him inside a stone. From then on he could see the light only when summoned by the stone's owner. Summoners could use Gilleot's power to control the emotions of others, but they became the object of envy of those around them.
