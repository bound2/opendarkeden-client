This Chakram takes its form from a talisman in the Black Pullet, a grimoire of black magic written in France around the 19th century. According to the Black Pullet, this talisman imparts knowledge of the demons of the underworld so that one can thwart their plans. The heart of the Black Pullet is its description of talismans, each used for a particular purpose. It presents 22 talismans in all; they appear in no other ancient grimoire and are wholly original, probably created by the book's author. They are used by reciting a particular incantation while touching the talisman or wearing it on the body, and each talisman is said to be used differently. The talisman in question must be held in the hand in times of danger, which makes it a fitting talisman for a Chakram, always held in the hand.
