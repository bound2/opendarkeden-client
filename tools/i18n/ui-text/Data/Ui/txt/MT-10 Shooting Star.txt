Modified from Buell's MT series shotgun, the MT-10 is a non-automatic single-shot shotgun that does not perform especially well in accuracy or power. However, its failure rate is too low to believe it has been in production since 1950, and it is known as a gun loved by many Slayers.
Unlike other guns, a shotgun can disable a Vampire with a single shot, which is why it is the weapon mostly used by American Slayers. However, beyond a certain range it does little damage, and powerful Vampires often attack Slayers during reloads, so it is not a weapon recommended to rookie Slayers.
