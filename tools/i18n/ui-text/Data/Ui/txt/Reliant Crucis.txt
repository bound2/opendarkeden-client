The Greek Orthodox Church was once a branch of the Catholic faith, but it refused to recognize the Pope, holding that the office itself was too political. This left the Holy See and the Greek Orthodox Church in an unspoken rivalry. Even against the Vampires, their common enemy, the Orthodox Church gave no official support, since the campaign was led by the Holy See, but it placed no restrictions on the independent activities of its monks. These monks showed who they were with an equal-armed cross unlike the common cross, and as the cross of monks acting on their own convictions it was named Reliant.
