Carnelian, the stone of Napoleon's seal, is a red gem that appears in the Bible under the name 'odem', and it is said that whoever carries it will have all their wishes come true. The name carnelian comes from the Latin carnem, meaning 'fresh', and true to its name this red stone was prized for its power to create energy and vitality and to give fresh energy to body and mind. The prophet Muhammad, later founder of Islam, always wore a carved carnelian seal as an amulet on the little finger of his right hand. When the famous Muslim Jafar declared that anyone who carries carnelian will have every wish come true, its standing became all the more absolute, and it is held extremely sacred among Muslims.
