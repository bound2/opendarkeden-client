Every vampire, when setting out on a journey, has its zombies carry a coffin to rest in. But in unexpected situations - a long battle with a powerful enemy, or a long trip in search of fresh blood that leaves it too far from its resting place - if the sun is about to rise, a vampire can instantly dig into the ground and hide its body. A spot where a vampire has burrowed in is hard to notice for anyone less than very observant, but expert vampire hunters are said to be able to sense the distinctive evil aura a vampire gives off.
