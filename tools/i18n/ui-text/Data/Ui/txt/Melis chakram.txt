A chakram holding the power of Melis, the spirit of sorrow; it is said to grow ever sharper as it absorbs the pain and grief of its victims. Its four blades are each set at a slightly offset angle, so even a single blow deals a grievous wound. Since Melis is a spirit of the mind, she does not clash with the spirit stones of the elemental spirits. It is therefore said that by swapping spirit stones one can wield elemental attacks without any loss of power.
