When this blade first came to Eslania, people took it for just one of countless blades. Its only distinguishing feature was the ring at its tip, and in shape it was an ordinary curved blade. But they soon changed their minds. Many high-ranking Vampires fell to this blade, including leader-class Vampires.
Brought in from Tibet, this blade bore rings at its tip, each with a special power to suppress a Vampire's strength. The rings make a distinctive sound with every strike, and its only drawback is its slow attack speed.
