The Alseides, nymphs of the forest, are so nimble that even strapping men could never catch them, so swift are their legs. The Alseides' leaf is a means of communication among spirits: one writes a message on the leaf and borrows the power of the Sylph, the spirit of the wind, to deliver it to the recipient. The Ousters use it to send news of their well-being or urgent orders.
