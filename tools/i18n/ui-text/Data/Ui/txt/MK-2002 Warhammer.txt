The Warhammer, the pinnacle of the MK series, is a rifle in the line of the G2000 and G2. However, its internal design was simplified to regain the MK series' original strength of low failure rates, and a redesigned barrel gave it explosive destructive power. But when it came out the POLAN series dominated the market, so it did not win much popularity, and in the end development of the MK series stopped; it is said that many MK fans mourned.
