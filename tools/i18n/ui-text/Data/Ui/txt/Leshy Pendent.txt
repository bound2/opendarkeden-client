The leshy is the Slavic forest spirit. Every forest is home to at least one leshy. Folklore says their blood is blue, so their cheeks and lips are blue as well. Their shaggy hair, their beards reaching to the knees, their thick eyebrows and their eyes are all green.
They cast no shadow and leave no footprints. In the forest they are all-powerful, able to grow taller than a cedar or shrink lower than the grass.
When someone walking in the forest feels they are being followed and suddenly turns around, the spirits vanish in an instant, so they are never seen.
In winter they die for a time or simply disappear. Then, when spring comes, they grow bright and cheerful as if reborn, making buds sprout on the trees and flowers bloom in the thickets.
