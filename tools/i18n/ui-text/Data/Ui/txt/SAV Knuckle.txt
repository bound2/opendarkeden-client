Combining the strengths of both knuckle and claw, this weapon blends the sharpness of a claw with the attack power of a knuckle. It was originally made by SAV, a human weapons maker, but right after release it was shunned for having been made purely for killing. When production stopped, the leftover stock found its way into Vampire hands through illegal channels, and it now sells at high prices among the many Vampires drawn to its practicality and power.
