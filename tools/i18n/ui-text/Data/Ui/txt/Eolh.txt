Beneficus, the legendary Vampire mage, developed a spell to seal part of his power in an orb just before his destruction. He wished that power alone to last forever even if his Vampire body perished, but the spell was left unfinished when he was destroyed. Some Vampires who inherited Beneficus' blood leave behind an orb inscribed with runes just before their destruction, and these orbs instill greater strength in Vampires. The rune left by the archmage Beneficus is believed never to have been found, but rumor has it that it fell into the hands of a Vampire Master.
