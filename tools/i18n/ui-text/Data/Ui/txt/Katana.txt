An uchigatana (Katana), a blade that came from Japan. As the name says, it is a blade for striking down. A young man from the East brought it into Eslania and, with his own swordsmanship, once swept all the Vampires out of northern Eslania. With its sturdy edge and balanced curve, the whole blade is strong and keen.
Each smith had his own special forging method, so prices were set by the master who made the blade, and its rarity made everyone long to own one at least once. One Slayer went so far as to say, "When I hold this blade I feel so steady it is as if the blade and I are one body, and its power cuts through anything." New methods of attack and defense were even developed with this blade.
