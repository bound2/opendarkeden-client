While an underground tunnel was being dug in the north of Mount Rodin to build an EVE supply base, a rock wall collapsed in disaster. During the rescue, thousands of corpses were found, along with a great many scrolls written in an ancient language.
These scrolls, thought to date from the late 14th century, are reported to hold the secrets of resurrection magic left by the black magic scholar Yobel. Unlike an ordinary Resurrection Scroll, this is an advanced scroll that also fully cures some abnormal conditions upon revival, and it is said to work not only on humans but on Vampires and animals as well.
