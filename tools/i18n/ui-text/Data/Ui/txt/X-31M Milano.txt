The "Milano" is an old sniper rifle that began winning the affection of European snipers in the '70s. It is a sniper rifle from Miera, the famous Italian military arms maker, temporarily modified to fire VK rounds, and it was well received because it performed reasonably well. In the '80s, however, as successor models appeared one after another, it was reduced to a classic weapon. It has average accuracy and firepower.
