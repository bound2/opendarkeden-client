A necklace imbued with the energy of the moon, bearing a crescent-shaped ornament with golden stripes; it is also called the 'Moon Goddess'. Because the necklace is influenced by the lunar cycle, on the evening of the third day of the lunar month, when the moon is 3 days old and appears as a crescent, the pendant takes on a reddish glow. (On the third day of the lunar month, the crescent is said to look reddish because of light reflected from the Earth.) It is the most superior pendant in defense and durability.
