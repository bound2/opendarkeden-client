A wristlet holding a vine of belladonna, said to have been a favorite of witches. Belladonna is the plant medieval witches used to fly through the sky, and it is said to be able to summon demons. Its beautiful black berries yield atropine, a substance the sorceress Medea was especially fond of, but taken in excess it becomes a very dangerous poison. Yet the plant's own magic is so strong that mages sometimes add it when making their magic tools. Because it is dangerous, though, such items are deliberately made to be used for a shorter time than other magic tools.
