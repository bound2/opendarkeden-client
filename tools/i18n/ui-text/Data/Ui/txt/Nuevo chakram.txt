A chakram that expresses, in images of overlapping blades and flames, the power of the 'Taiji' symbol, seen as the reality holding the energy to create all things in the universe. The 'Taiji' is the fundamental reality of the universe. The Taiji changes to become yin and yang. Heaven, earth and all things are made of yang and yin. Yin usually represents the feminine energy of the universe, and yang the masculine. Yin and yang thus symbolize every opposition in nature: man and woman, heaven and earth, sun and moon, strength and softness, and so on. As yin and yang change and interact, all the phenomena of the universe change too. The only thing that does not change is the principle of change itself: that all things keep changing according to the principle of yin and yang.
