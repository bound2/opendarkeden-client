A Circlet bearing the seal of the moon, a talisman that opens locked doors. This seal serves a very practical purpose: it summons the spirit of the moon to open locked doors. There are 7 kinds of these seals in all, named after the sun, the moon and five planets of the solar system, and according to astrology and magic these seven stars each govern a particular part of a person's life. Each seal is therefore said to take effect in its own area.
