'I.N.R.I' is the title that the Roman governor Pontius Pilate fixed above Jesus's cross, naming his crime: "Jesus of Nazareth, King of the Jews". The first letters of the Latin Iesus Nazarenus Rex Iudaeorum were taken to make INRI and placed above the crucifix; it was a charge meant to mock and kill Jesus, but in the end it became the Gentiles' own confession of who Jesus was. The cross means the cross on which Jesus was nailed and died, a symbol of suffering and death and at the same time of salvation.
