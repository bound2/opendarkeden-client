A shield shaped like a unicorn. The unicorn shields used by medieval knights stood for Christ in the Catholic sense. Before going to war, knights are said to have received the priests' blessing and then carried this shield with the unicorn on it. The present-day shield is produced by EVE itself. Made of synthetic resin, it is light and easy to use, but to make up for its weak durability and defense it is given a special treatment before use.
