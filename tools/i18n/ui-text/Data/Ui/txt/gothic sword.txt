The Slayers who had hunted Vampires since ancient times wanted swords suited to their original purpose and used many swords. Among them they found the one that appealed most to their sensibilities and was the most natural and realistic: the gothic sword. Mostly used by Germanic Slayers, it inflicted such great and cruel carnage that Slayers from other regions sometimes shunned it.
Unlike earlier swords, the gothic sword kept its balance despite its broad blade and weight, and both the blade and the hilt were used in combat; its forging method and shape differed from those of the medieval and early modern periods. Countless grooves of varied shapes are carved into the blade, forming beautiful patterns, but each served to inflict greater wounds on the enemy. Carrying the individual spiritual strength of each Slayer, this sword earned the stigma of being a "barbaric" sword, but it is one of the few swords that can be trusted in battle against Vampires.
