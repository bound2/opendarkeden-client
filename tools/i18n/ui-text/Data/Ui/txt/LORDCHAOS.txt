Wielding two-handed swords, they recall the gladiators of Roman times. An ancient document describing a battle with a Lord Chaos records that 'when a Lord Chaos brought down its two-handed sword, the sound of the air splitting made the Slayers tremble in pain and terror, as if their eardrums were tearing'.

The Lord Chaos is called the master of chaos who rules over chaos, and wherever it passes, it is said to leave only withered corpses drained of all their blood.
