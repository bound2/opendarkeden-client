A ring symbolizing the point of light that appears in clouds or mist directly opposite the sun, it gives a Vampire the strength to endure the power of the sun. During a total solar eclipse, when the moon completely covers the sun, a 'diamond ring' is said to form; the shape it makes matches that of this ring, and the power of darkness brings a Vampire's strength to its peak. (Depending on the distance between the sun and the moon during the eclipse) the wearer's power grows for the brief moment of up to about 8 minutes when totality is at its height (the darkness itself lasts about an hour). In one saros cycle of roughly 18 years, 41 solar eclipses occur, and during the 10 total eclipses among them Vampires are said to wield their greatest power.
