The priests who had long handled the effective treatment of attacks and wounds suffered by warriors from the rear always lamented that their numbers fell absurdly short of the demand. Forced to watch helplessly as so many Slayers beyond their reach cried out for rescue, they sought a new way, and after long research succeeded in making the Holy Potion, which heals even faster than the existing Ampul. The Holy Potion is a precious potion made from holy water blessed with a priest's healing prayer.
