Naphne, a spirit dwelling in the primeval forest, was a guardian in the form of a long-tailed bird who watched over every corner of the forest. The forest in his care was hidden from every eye, and even if an outsider tried to break in, it remained safely protected. In times of danger Naphne wrapped the forest tightly in his long tail feathers, and no one could easily break through them. If an intruder was too powerful to stop, Naphne could wrap the forest in his tail and carry it off to a hidden place, and so shake off the intruder.
