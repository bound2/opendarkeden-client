Before RESS acquired Buell, it once commissioned a modified super shotgun that could kill large mammals in a single shot. RESS, then secretly conducting biological research on Vampires, needed more actual Vampire tissue, so it had to form a mercenary unit to capture Vampires alive. But the mercenaries were wiped out, and RESS reportedly never extracted any useful information. At the time only a few test models were built, and no production line ever actually ran. After Buell was acquired, however, the test model was finally mass-produced under the name Alligator.
