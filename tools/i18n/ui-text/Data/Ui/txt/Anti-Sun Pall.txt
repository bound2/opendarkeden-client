Made of special steel wire and leather, this Coat is recognized among Vampires as the finest of Coats. It wraps the whole body and offers the best defense and durability even in battle against Slayers. Its magic is so strong that it is called the privilege of powerful Vampires, and young Vampires are said to be unable to wear it lightly. Although the material feels weighty, its strengths are that it is light and easy to move in.
