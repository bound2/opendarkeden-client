A Circlet holding a brook familiar. Familiar is the general name for the spirits or animals that work at a sorcerer's command. Among familiars, spirits without physical form are specifically called agathions. Like the genie of the lamp in the Arabian Nights, they are sealed in bottles, rings and the like and could be called out when needed. Since they could be used for anything, witches are said to have even had familiars wait on them. That magic has since been lost, however, and the Circlet now serves only through the protective power of the familiar held within it.
