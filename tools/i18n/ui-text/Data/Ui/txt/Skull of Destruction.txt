A cursed earring, a relic of the Liches, symbols of immortality, whose name means total destruction or total death. The earring looks like gold on the outside, but it is actually a metal of darkness created by an alchemist. He made the earring from this metal of darkness by sacrificing his own body for his wife, who died after being mistaken for a witch and burned in a witch hunt, and the skull hanging from it is said to be carved from his own child's skull. A Vampire who wears this earring feels nothing but an eternal instinct for destruction.
