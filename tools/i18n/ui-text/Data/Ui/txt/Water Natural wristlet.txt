A wristlet said to be made from a piece of the World Tree, the tree at the center of the world. The World Tree has come down in myths and folk tales widespread among many pre-literate peoples, especially in Asia, Australia and North America. They depict the tree as the vertical axis connecting heaven and earth. In biblical terms it corresponds to the Tree of Knowledge. Legends of this vertical tree of wisdom say it reaches from the earth into the sky, the link between the world of the gods and the world of men. There are also folk tales of oracles, judgments and prophecies delivered beneath it. However, the World Tree has never been shown to exist, and although they know it is a sales pitch, those new to magic are said to be fond of this wristlet, taking it as a pleasant lie.
