A mace made entirely of silver, identical in basic shape to the Iron Mace. It adds the power of silver to the basic power of the Iron Mace, making it a dependable mace. The shaft of the Silver Mace is made of two rods twisted together, and the head is worked from silver with additives, together with the symbol of its owners' church.
Each region used maces of different materials, but the most widely used was "silver".
