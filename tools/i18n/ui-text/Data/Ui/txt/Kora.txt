The kora is a sword originally made for beheadings. For the intimidation and practicality it shows in close combat and the ease of carrying out summary executions, it was bestowed on the royal guard of Nepal. Unusually, it is sharpened on both sides, so it can be drawn back like a sickle to cut with the back edge. There is also a hole at the tip of the blade for punishments that cut off fingers, toes and other parts of the body.
