A cross believed to have been made in the late 10th century. In 1817 the English explorer James Clumming found it on the road to the holy city of Jerusalem on the West Bank of the Jordan. It was named the Jordanian Cross after the place where it was found, and it symbolizes the merciful Christ. Shortly after its discovery it was taken to England and was later formally handed over to the Holy See. It is also called the Cross of Mercy.
