Short for Objective Individual Combat Weapon, one of the most recently developed modern weapons. British-made and double-barreled, it is fitted with an infrared scope, a laser sight, a bayonet and more. The gun breaks down into 7 parts, and being double-barreled it carries a separate magazine for each barrel. To provide state-of-the-art weapons fit for the war against Vampires, E.V.E. plans to issue it after a testing phase; so far it has been issued on trial only to some officers deployed at the front.
