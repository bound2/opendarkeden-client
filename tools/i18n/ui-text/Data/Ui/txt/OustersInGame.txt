Ousters skills consist of close combat techniques and Fire, Water and Earth magic.
To learn Ousters skills, you spend the Skill Points you receive when you level up.
Ousters learn skills and magic in the skill tree window.
You raise a skill's level by continuing to spend Skill Points on it.
To use a skill, bring it up in the skill window and right-click the target.
Skills are learned with the Skill Points you collect as you level up.
As a skill's level rises, you get Damage and To Hit bonuses.
Skill level shows how well you have mastered a skill, and it rises as you spend Skill Points.
The techniques you can use depend on the weapon you use.
For physical attack and defense in close combat, you must wear a 'Chakram'.
To use elemental magic, you must wear a 'Wristlet'.
Press Control+H to view the help.
Press Control+K to view the skill tree.
Press Control+I to check your own information.
Ousters can travel quickly using summoning stones.
Ousters can warp to the place they want using the 'Earth Spirit Horns' found all over the map.
To recover HP, use a recovery item, or move near an 'Earth Spirit Horn' and you recover automatically.
Experience (EXP) : rises when Ousters kill other creatures.
When several players attack one monster, Experience is shared according to the Damage each dealt to the creature.
EP is spent when you use magic and skills and when you create recovery items.
You can refill EP with Absorb Soul.
Recovery items can be placed in the Armsband window and used from there.
To use a skill, turn on the skill window with Control+S, then right-click.
To set spell hotkeys, press S, then assign them to F7~F12.
At character creation, an Ousters' stats total 45.
