Made by working the bones of ancient giants, beings known to have made up the ancient world. They are also called fallen spirits, and their power is said to have been twice that of ordinary spirits. Because of their evil aura, a seal of purification is engraved on the surface to protect the user from side effects. But the aura is so strong that anyone who uses it even once, however pure their soul, is tainted by corruption and can hardly return to their original purity.
