Protective gear used by the priests of the "Vatican Knights", a Slayer organization directly under the Catholic Church. The hand is made of leather covered with sheet metal, stressing both defense and mobility. As befits a priest's gear, it is also lavishly decorated. However, the wrist is made of white cloth, which weakens its defense. On the white cloth at the wrist a verse of scripture is written in red: "I give you the wisdom and power to overcome the devil; go forth and fight bravely! I will always be with you." Its defense is lower, but it is a glove imbued with the power of the Holy Spirit.
