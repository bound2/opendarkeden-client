Used for Vampire teleportation, this bluish gem is a memory device. When it memorizes a location, the energy of red blood comes out of the stone and wraps around the user's body, recognizes the user, and then burrows deep underground at the place to be memorized. For that moment the stone, having recognized the place, glows red-hot. To go back there, take out the gem and open a door with Bloody Tunnel; the door to the memorized place opens and you can travel there. Each gem can designate one destination zone and can be charged up to 5 times.
