A sword 115-140 cm long. In ordinary combat the Light Sword was too light to bring down an armored enemy, and the War Sword, though destructive, was too heavy for consecutive attacks or for attacking while carrying a shield. Designed with this in mind, it can be used with one hand or two and is built purely for serious combat; because it broke with conventional ideas of the sword, it came to be called the bastard sword.
The strength of this sword is how easily it adapts to the situation; in close fights with savage Vampires, fighters would even throw away their shields and brawl. Slayers engraved prayers for divine protection on the blade or decorated the grip with the fangs of Vampires they had killed. It is a good sword for Slayers facing Vampires with their speed and stamina.
