The Altena Wristlet (215) is a Wristlet for high-grade advanced Ousters of Advancement grade 65 and above. It can be obtained by enchanting an existing Advancement Wristlet with an Old Drop.
Unlike other higher Advancement items, it has no hidden or basic options, but its magic damage is +30 stronger than that of the grade 1 Advancement Wristlet.
