The first model of POLAN, the rifle series independently developed by Karastoyev, a name anyone who knows even a little about rifles has heard at least once. Karastoyev spent his youth in the Soviet Union until the rise of the Vampires cost him his whole family, and at 30 he finally fled into exile in Europe. With the full backing of the Holy See he began developing rifles, and he gave his very first model the name "POLAN", after his wife, who had been killed by Vampires.
