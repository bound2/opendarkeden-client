This white star, a fitting emblem of the greatest purity, is said to be exchangeable for money, which everyone needs.

The White Star is an event item that can be exchanged for money with the NPCs below, depending on your race and location.

 Field Headquarters NPC : Kaiser
 Warrior Guild NPC : Conan
 Soldier Guild NPC : Smith
 Priest Guild NPC : Stoyanov
 Vampire Village NPC : Vrykolakas

* When you have 1 or more stars, go to Kaiser, Conan, Smith, Stoyanov, Vrykolakas or the like, select 'I want to exchange my stars for money' and left-click to exchange this item for money.
