A sword imbued with an unknown power, marked by the war goddess Bellona engraved on its hilt and blade. No one knows where it came from, but legend has it that any army that went to war behind this sword was sure to win. Beautiful to look at, it is a sword of truly cruel fate: whoever grasped it was intoxicated by its boundless power and wanted to keep cutting down enemies, and countless heroes fell beneath her. The sword's true power does not come out from merely swinging it.
