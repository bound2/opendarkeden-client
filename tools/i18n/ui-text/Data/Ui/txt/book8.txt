By day they sleep in their coffins; at night they rise, slip into houses and suck the blood of sleeping people. The victims whose blood is taken also become vampires after death and wander in search of new victims. Some vampires strangle their prey before drinking its blood, while others suck the blood of their victims as they sleep. Vampires drink blood because blood is the source of life force: by making the life force contained in the blood their own, they keep themselves from wasting away. A vampire's bite leaves tooth marks, and its victims are said to bear wounds like two needle pricks around the neck.
