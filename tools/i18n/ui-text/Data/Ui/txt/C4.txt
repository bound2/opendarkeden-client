The best-known plastic explosive, with considerable explosive power even among explosives. Made with RDX (Cyclonite) and a plasticizer, it is used as a military explosive. As a putty-like explosive it can be molded into shape, and it is sometimes used still in its wrapping with just a detonator inserted. It usually comes as a rectangular block about 28 cm long and about 5 cm wide and high. Its relative effectiveness factor is 1.34, a full 34% stronger than TNT (trinitrotoluene), and C4 detonates at 8300 m/s.
