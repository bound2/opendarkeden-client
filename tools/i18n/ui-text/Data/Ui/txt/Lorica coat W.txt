A coat that maximizes mobility and defense, with a lorica (armor worn by Roman soldiers in the age of the kings, which in the Republican era became the armor of officers; it is leather armor faced with gold fittings for defense and splendour) fitted on the chest to boost the defense the earlier coat lacked in close combat. It fixes the discomfort of the long length and narrow cut with a double coat on the upper arms and wide slits on the lower half, making it more mobile.
