The aspis is a round shield that originated in the Doris region of Greece and dates back to before the 7th century BC. The boss of an aspis found on the island of Cyprus is a conical protrusion called the omphalos. The shields came in various sizes, and later examples, finely gilded and engraved with various patterns, survive today; modeled on them, the shield has been adapted into a form that allows both defense and attack, and is still in use.
