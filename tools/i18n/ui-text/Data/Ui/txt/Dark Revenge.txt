The number of long-lived vampires never grows, because even a vampire can meet with an untimely accident or fall to the Slayers who hunt it relentlessly. Reckless but mighty vampires who see a vampire of their blood die before their eyes sometimes feel a kinship that hardly suits them, and burn with rage. Through joint research with intelligent vampires, who learned that such rage gave them far more power than usual, they became able to gain this astonishing power at will by burning their own blood.
