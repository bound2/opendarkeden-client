The vampire bats that so often appear in horror films live mainly in the tropics of the Americas. Though smaller than a human hand, vampire bats feed on the blood of cattle, birds and even people. A vampire bat usually lands softly on a sleeping animal, makes a tiny cut in its skin with razor-sharp teeth, then curls its tongue so that the blood runs into its mouth. In fact it does not suck the blood at all but laps it up very quickly. A single vampire bat is said to consume about 11 liters of blood a year.
