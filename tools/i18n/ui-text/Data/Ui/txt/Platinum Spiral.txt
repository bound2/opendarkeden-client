A swirl-patterned ring made of platinum. The spiral on its outer surface is a blend of many forces and represents the sun, the moon, the air, the cycles of the cosmos, and the source of power and life. Since this ring was said to grant some power to its wearer the moment it was put on, many people even went to war to win it. Nothing is actually known of that power, but it is a ring believed to grant power.
