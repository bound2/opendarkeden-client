The gruz is a mace widely used in northern India. Many kinds of gruz appeared in the late 16th century, at the height of the Mughal Empire; they have no fixed characteristic shape, but the name refers to maces with large, unusually shaped heads. Though the gruz was a formidable weapon, it disappeared as firearms advanced tactics. All that remain today are royal treasures, lavishly gilded and engraved with beautiful patterns or carefully designed.
