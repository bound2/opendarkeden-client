A circlet that was once the trophy of a wight, a walking corpse. A wight is an evil spirit that has passed into the corpse of a king or queen laid in a burial mound with their treasure; its cold, mummified body is decked with ornaments, so its rings and gold jewelry jingle as it walks. It always dwells inside the tomb, but, perhaps to swell its ranks, it casts a curse on anyone who comes calling to lure them into the mound and kill them. It is said, though, that it cannot lay a hand on a hero with a fearless heart. If you are truly brave, inside this tomb you will find not the wight but only the precious metals strewn across the floor.
