This unusual claw, with its blades facing inward, is a cruel instrument of execution and is said to have been used for torture as well. By adjusting the tension of the blades and hanging the head inside them, it slowly peeled the skin from the victim's face until death. The small spike in the center did not even allow a scream of pain and made breathing difficult. No one knows exactly when it came to be used as a weapon, but despite its inefficient-looking form it has won renown on the battlefield as a deadly weapon.
