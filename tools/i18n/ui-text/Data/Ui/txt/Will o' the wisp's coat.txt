The Will o' the Wisp is a spirit of light, created by the gods with the power to control light. Apart from giving off light it has no physical power, but its light can grow bright enough to blind a person in an instant. This energy of light has served as a scourge to Vampires and as a good defense that confuses weak human sight. Because of this power of the Will o' the Wisp, the Ousters are said to have long kept the custom of always carrying objects holding this spirit's energy as amulets.
