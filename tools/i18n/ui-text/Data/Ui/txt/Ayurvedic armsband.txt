An Armsband symbolizing the Kundalini serpent, which comes from Tantra, one of the branches of Indian Hinduism. In Hinduism, Kundalini means a power hidden within us humans, a kind of latent potential. The word Kundalini itself means 'coiled' or 'hidden'. This hidden power is said to be a kind of spiritual energy, and awakening it lets one do many things that once seemed impossible. The word comes from Tantra, the mystical school of Hinduism. In Tantra, the serpent called 'Kundalini' symbolizes life force and spiritual energy, and patterns representing them are worked into armor and accessories.
