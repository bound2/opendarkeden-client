Legend says this hard knuckle glove, made by setting jewels into a heavy metal gauntlet, was made for a medieval Scottish noble to flaunt his wealth. The craftsman did his utmost so that the knuckle would be more than an ornament, but for missing his delivery date he lost his life at the noble's hands. The genuine Might Knuckle is kept in a museum, and its intricate craftsmanship and formidable attack power have spawned many imitations.
