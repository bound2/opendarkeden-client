An ordinary rapier has a very long, narrow blade. Because the rapier is a thrusting sword, with luck it can pierce a Vampire's heart in one stroke. Since it could go for a single killing blow instead of clumsy cuts, it was popular for a time, but in melees, and especially in fights against hardy Vampires, the blade suffered heavy damage. To remedy this weakness came the Broad Rapier, a slightly wider and sturdier sword.
The whole rapier can be silver-plated, and it deals great damage when it pierces the heart. It was used mainly by Slayers skilled in swordsmanship.
