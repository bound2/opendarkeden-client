An armsband derived from a vever, the symbolic design used to call up spirits in the secret rites of Voodoo, the folk religion of Haiti. This vever is the design for the serpent god named Damballah. In Voodoo, the folk religion of the island of Haiti, such patterns are used as ritual devices for calling up the spirits known as the Loa. Whoever summons a Loa spirit is said to become an unbending person who yields to no threat.
