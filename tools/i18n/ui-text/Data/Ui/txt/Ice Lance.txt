A higher technique of Grupein, the ice spirit, which hurls powerful shards of ice through the air to attack the enemy for the spirit caller who summoned it. It is an intermediate frost spell whose power depends on the caster's ability.

A skill designed for water Ousters, who lack a long-range attack; it strikes from several directions so the target's facing matters less. Damage increases with INT, and damage and range grow as the skill level rises. Mastering the skill grants a substantial bonus.
