A Vampire Coat said to have been favored by ancient Vampires who honed great magic power. After long devotion to magic, they needed protective gear holding magic power to make up for their weakened bodies, and this Coat is said to have been specially made to meet that need. Handed down for thousands of years, it may look like a worn-out Coat at first glance, but the Vampires who own it regard it instead as a symbol of having fought through countless battlefields.
