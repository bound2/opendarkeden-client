Modeled on the Holy Grail, the cup of Christ's holy blood, this Grail Mace was kept by a council in Vatican City. Its exact date is unknown, but it is said that Pope John I began making it during his reign in the 1900s and that it took its final form after ten years of repeated revisions. The Calix Mace is said to be used only by a few clergy baptized by the Pope.
