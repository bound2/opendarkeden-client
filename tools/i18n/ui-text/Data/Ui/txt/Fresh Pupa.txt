The Arapa worm, which lives in the Castalo highlands, is also called the vanity worm because it produces deep blue gems. It spits out one deep blue gem each time it passes an instar. Their pupae are said to hold the power to restore lost bodily vigor. By communing with them, the Ousters instantly heal their wounds and recover their strength. They engrave these small sacrificed souls into their own souls forever.
