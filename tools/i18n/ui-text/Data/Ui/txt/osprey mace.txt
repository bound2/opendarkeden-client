An advanced form of the Pungo Mace, a mace with an osprey figure on the end of its shaft. The figure, carved down to every feather, gives the impression of a work of art rather than a weapon. Those struck by it are said to be overcome by hallucinations and terror and to die slowly. It is said to be modeled on the figure of an osprey that stood on a pillar at the underground entrance of an Egyptian pyramid.
