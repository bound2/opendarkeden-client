Spirits are immortal beings; even if a spirit's physical form vanishes, its soul circulates without end. To be reborn, they place their souls in spirit stones and ceaselessly purify their minds and power. If the pure power that flows out of a spirit stone is infused into a weapon or a magic spell, that medium becomes lastingly imbued with the spirit's power. This spirit stone can infuse the power of the water spirit Elquines.
