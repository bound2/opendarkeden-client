"Let no man therefore judge you in meat, or in drink, or in respect of an holyday, or of the new moon, or of the sabbath days: which are a shadow of things to come; but the body is of Christ."
The Episcopal Cross, the bishop's cross, has one more bar above the crossbeam. It is a cross brimming with the holy power of bishops who have long prevailed in their struggle against themselves and the temptations of evil.
