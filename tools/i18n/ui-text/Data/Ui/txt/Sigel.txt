A Bead left behind by the legendary Vampire mage Veneficus at the cost of his life, while he was developing a spell to trap part of his power in an orb. After his death it passed to his daughter Levia, but she was later murdered in a mysterious incident and the Bead's whereabouts became unknown. Rumour had it that Nosferatu, one of the Vampire Masters of the time, was behind it, but that was forgotten with the passing years. Left by a legendary mage, this Bead grants almost limitless magic power.
