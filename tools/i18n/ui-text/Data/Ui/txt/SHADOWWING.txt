A vampire that floats through the air using a unique technique called the Wings of Darkness. Normally it merely hovers a little above the ground, but it is said to be able to rise high into the air as well. It is fairly intelligent, and most Slayers who enter a Shadow Wing's sight are said to have little hope of escaping with their lives. The Shadow Wing can cast spells even while floating in the air, and it is known for moving fast.
