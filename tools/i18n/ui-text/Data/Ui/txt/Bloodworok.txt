Known as the 'Blood Sorcerers', these vampires are hard for Slayers to face. Their greed for blood knows no bounds, and they are said to drain blood at the slightest opening. When a Warlock drains a large amount of blood from a victim, it can even create a mutation like the Mutant.

They move with light, soft steps; a Slayer who senses one close by is already no longer of this world.
