An improved suit made of special urethane fiber. The inner and outer layers are specially treated to further improve durability, and the attached plates are also specially treated to strengthen them. A strength of this highly durable suit is that it does not deform easily under the Slayers' basic weapons. But its defense remains unimproved, so Vampires wearing it are said to often suffer light wounds. For this reason defense-boosting magic is usually cast on the suit. Made with gold thread, the suit also has a touch of splendour.
