Humanity's greatest strength lies in its vast intelligence capability, built on advanced technology. On a battlefield where the situation can change at any moment, getting information quickly affects not only personal safety but the whole course of the fight. The Info Scrap is a device that helps gather and analyze such information, linking up with the satellites of many nations to obtain fast, accurate intelligence. But as expensive equipment it is hard for an ordinary soldier to come by, and it trades at a very high price.
