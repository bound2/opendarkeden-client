Feoh represents livestock. To the ancient Germanic peoples livestock meant wealth, so in societies before a money economy cattle were a valuable, a form of wealth that grew with care. In esoteric lore, Feoh also symbolises Audhumla, the primeval cow of Norse myth. Audhumla was born in the ice before the world was created and is said to have nursed the primeval giant Ymir. From this, Feoh can be said to express the principle of growth.
