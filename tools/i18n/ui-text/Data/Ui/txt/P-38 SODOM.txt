"SODOM" is known as the most versatile rifle of the POLAN series. It shares over 80% of its parts with the P-61S and is said to have performed so well that it earned perfect marks in field tests with special forces. A variety of accessory kits exist for the "SODOM", for adjusting firepower and improving accuracy in day and night shooting. It was popular enough to be adopted by most special forces, yet the story goes that Karastoyev himself was never satisfied with it.
