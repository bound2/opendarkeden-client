A Pendant in which two serpents, left and right, encircle a round disc, with a skull in the center where the two serpents meet. The two serpents stand for dualistic opposites that are ultimately united, healing and poison, sickness and health, and are also said to represent the complementary nature of the two forces in the universe. Inside the central disc is another circle inscribed with ancient Egyptian script, and each point of the heptagon inside it is said to be linked to one of the seven deadly sins. The Pendant resembles the staff of an ancient Egyptian high priest.
