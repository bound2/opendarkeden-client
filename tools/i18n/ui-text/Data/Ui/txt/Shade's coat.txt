Shade, the spirit of darkness, has the opposite attribute to the Will O' the Wisp. The god, judging that rest was impossible because of the Will O' the Wisps present everywhere in the world, created Shade so that no Will O' the Wisp could exist wherever Shade was, making rest possible for living things. Shade is also called the spirit of rest. Holding a power of darkness that seems to swallow everything, this spirit was a summoning forbidden to all spirit mages. There was no way to control its immense power, so even the greatest spirit mages could not handle Shade lightly. After much deliberation the spirit mages chose to divide Shade's dangerously strong power, imbuing their belongings with it and constantly controlling that power with their own magic. Objects holding Shade's power are hard to find, and using one is said to be so dangerous that you must stake your life on it.
