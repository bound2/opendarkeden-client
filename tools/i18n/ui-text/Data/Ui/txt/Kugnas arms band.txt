An armsband shaped like the Dharma Chakra, the Wheel of Dharma, the signature symbol of Buddhism, showing the eight paths to enlightenment. It carries an image of harmony and reconciliation. The word 'Dharmachakra' is Sanskrit. Rendered into Chinese characters, dharma becomes 'law' and chakra becomes 'wheel', giving 'Wheel of the Law'. Simply put, the Wheel of the Law is 'the wheel of truth'. It is said that only those who have grasped the truth of the true self can possess it.
