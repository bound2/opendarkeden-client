Combat Help

In DKx Online, three different races - Slayers, Vampires and Ousters - can fight in many ways, using the various skills and magic of each class.
Slayers cannot grow their characters without a weapon equipped. Vampires and Ousters also use weapons, but their growth is not
restricted by them.
Hold the mouse button on a monster to keep attacking until it dies. Click with Caps Lock on to automatically attack monsters within attack range around you.
