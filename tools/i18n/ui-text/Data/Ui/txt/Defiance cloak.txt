The Vampires' coats, so thick they look stifling, were a tradition stubbornly kept by the Vampire Masters, protecting bodies vulnerable to light while displaying their dignity. But in Eslania, where the fear of light has vanished, such heavy coats became a burden. Young Vampires came to prefer clothing built for mobility over the dignity the Masters insist on, and this taste reveals a desire in their hearts to step out of the Masters' shadow.
