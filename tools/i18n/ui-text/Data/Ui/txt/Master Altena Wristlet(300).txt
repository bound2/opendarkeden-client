Master Altena Wristlet(300) is a wristlet usable by Ousters of Advancement level 300 or higher. This item is obtained by enchanting an existing Advancement wristlet with a Reliance Drop.
By default its magic damage is +50 higher than that of a first-grade Advancement wristlet, and it grows stronger still with the hidden options below.
