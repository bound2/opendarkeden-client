A blade curved like a crescent moon. The Moon Blade curves long, all the way down to the hilt; its blade grows thinner and more translucent toward the tip, and though very light it can deal a grievous wound with even a slight cut. Its material is highly elastic, and it is said to glow softly at night.
Only a few Slayers ever used this blade; it is hard to handle, and even its materials are hard to come by, making it very rare. The hilt is grooved to add strength to the gently curved blade. Rumor has it that it was forged from a meteorite that fell from the sky.
