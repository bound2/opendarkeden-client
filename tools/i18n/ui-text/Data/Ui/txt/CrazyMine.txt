Crazy Mine
A mix of Minesweeper and Bulls and Cows: you must open every tile that has no mine.
If you open a tile with a mine, you fail the game.
Left-click to select 3 tiles; the game shows how many of them hold mines and how many do not.
If all 3 selected tiles are free of mines, or all hold mines, it shows '3O or 3X'.
To open a tile without a mine, click the same tile twice.
To mark a mine, right-click to plant a flag on it.
Right-click a flag again to turn it into a question mark; right-click once more to remove the mark.
