The LM-700 'Kar' rifle is an improved M16 model by ZM-Weapon of Massachusetts, USA, made lighter and more accurate. M/L stands for Military/Law, meaning military and police use. Its great strengths are the accuracy to stop an enemy with a single shot and outstanding power. It uses the lower receiver of the M-16 rifle, but the upper receiver has Z-M's distinctive design. It uses a gas system called the op-rod, which lets the stock fold and at the same time allows a short barrel. The top of the LR-300 M/L rifle is flat, so a Weaver-type mount, electronic sights and a telescopic lens can all be fitted there. The trigger, charging handle and magazine release are the same as the M-16's, but it adopts the grip of the pistol called the "2004" for greater stability when firing, so anyone can use it easily; its high price, however, makes it hard to obtain.
