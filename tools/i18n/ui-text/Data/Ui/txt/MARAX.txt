This claw, with a splendid blade reminiscent of an angel's wing, was found in an underground ruin about 20km from Jerusalem's Old City. Marax is the name of a demon sealed away by Solomon, said to have been a gifted anatomist of the demon world. Once an angel who brought hope to the sick, he stepped into the world of darkness during Satan's uprising. He deceived humans with his beautiful looks into offering a living sacrifice every month, until the wise king Solomon sealed him deep underground. The weapon got its name because its white blade, with a reddish tinge, resembles the blood-stained wings of a fallen angel.
