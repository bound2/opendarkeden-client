A hand grenade of the kind commonly used in the army. It is the most common type of bomb and simple to use, since you only need to pull the safety pin, but it has the drawback of being of little use outside close combat. Once the fuse is triggered, the delay charge burns for 2-5 seconds and then ignites the explosive. For maximum lethality, pull the pin, let the lever fly off and throw it 2-3 seconds later: it bursts in mid-air and scatters fragments through 360 degrees.
