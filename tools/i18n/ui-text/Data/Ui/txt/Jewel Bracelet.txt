A bracelet set with jewels. It is the most dazzling of all Vampire items in existence. It is set with jewels of many colors, and their hues are lovely. But for all its splendour, it has no special feature compared with other items. No power dwells in the bracelet; it is purely decorative. It is said that the stones did not lack power from the start, but that their opposing powers collided and canceled each other out.
