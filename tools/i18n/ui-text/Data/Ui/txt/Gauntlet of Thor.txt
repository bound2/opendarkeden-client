Slayer gear called the Gauntlet of Thor. Thor, the ancient Germanic god, was the patron of farmers who wielded a great hammer and wore gloves; this is said to be modeled on the iron gloves he used. Up to the wrist it is made of iron, and above that of a copper-tin alloy, improving durability and protection. A lion is drawn on the upper part, marking its wearer as a brave warrior.
