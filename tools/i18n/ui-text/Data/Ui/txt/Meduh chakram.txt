The Meduh Chakram takes the form of a symbol that harmonizes the Dharma wheel, the chief symbol of Buddhism, with the Taiji, the chief symbol of Chinese religion, and it stands for harmony and reconciliation among people. Put simply, the Dharma wheel is the 'wheel of truth'. Truth here means the right path that leads humans to ultimate happiness, that is, to salvation. What draws this wheel to its center is the Taiji. Philosophically its three layers stand for 'heaven, earth and humanity'; from another angle they can be seen as affirmation, negation and the harmony of these two extremes. That they merge in a spiral is surely because life in this world unfolds as these three elements dynamically harmonize. The Taiji therefore signifies that happiness can be reached through harmony.
