Spirit callers who use the power of nature fully grasped the laws of nature and, learning to merge themselves with it, reached the stage of making their own form vanish. This skill is useful for keeping or closing the distance to an opponent, and it can even make you vanish in an instant, more than enough to bewilder the opponent.

A skill that instantly moves your character. Delay is reduced equally by INT and DEX, and range increases with skill level, the same for all Ousters. Mastering the skill grants a substantial bonus.
