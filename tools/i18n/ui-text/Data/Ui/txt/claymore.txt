Basically a kind of two-handed sword, close in length to the Light Sword. This quintessential Scottish sword was built to bear its weight as well as possible while keeping it nimble for cutting down opponents. A sword with a sharp point and keen edges, it was mostly used silver-plated by Slayers, and some had grips wrapped in silver wire. The ornament at the ends of the guard, with curved bars piercing a ring, carries the magical meaning of a prayer for victory in the fight against Vampires. Thin and springy for its weight, it inherits the 'cutting' ability of the longsword. It was loved less for its own power than because Slayers could easily bring their own techniques to bear with it, and because it was durable.
