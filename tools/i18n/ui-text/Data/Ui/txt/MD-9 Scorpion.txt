The MD-9 is the model that carries the pride of the MD series. It was made to fix the flaws of the older MD models, which were fussy for Slayers to maintain and had weaker firepower than some later guns. It is small and light and uses sVK rounds, giving it much stronger firepower. With its sturdy stock and specially treated barrel, the MD-9 is a weapon that can serve any purpose. The foregrip is very useful for keeping recoil down and staying on target during automatic fire.
