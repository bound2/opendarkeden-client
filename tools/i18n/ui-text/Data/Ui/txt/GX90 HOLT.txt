Made in Germany, the Holt is known as the world's most precise semi-automatic sniper rifle. It fires so accurately that 50 rounds at 300 m land within an 80 mm circle, and thanks to this extremely high accuracy it has become a byword for sniper rifles. However, its spent cases fly nearly 10 m from the firing point, so it tends to give away the sniper's position. Its scope performs best at 600 m, the ideal range for a sniper.
