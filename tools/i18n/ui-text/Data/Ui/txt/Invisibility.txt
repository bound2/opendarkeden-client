Have you ever walked a dark street at night and felt that someone at the end of an alley was watching you? Have you ever felt your skin crawl even though, however much you looked around, you could sense no one? If so, you have met a vampire. Powerful vampires are said to be able to hide their bodies behind a magical veil and move unseen by human eyes. Once invisible, vampires move without a trace, but they show themselves just before attacking a human. Yet the fine priests of the Vatican are said to be able to sense such invisible vampires and to hold a holy power that can even cancel the spell.
