The bracelet of a Vampire Master. It is made of pieces engraved with 8 special symbols in the runes of the early Germanic peoples. The script is over 2,000 years old, written in pictographs that give shape to individual words; besides the seven sins named in the Bible, namely greed, sloth, envy, lust, pride, wrath and gluttony, the remaining symbol stands for the thirst for blood. It is said that deciphering them reveals the key to unlocking ancient Vampire magic.
