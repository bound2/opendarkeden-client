The cutlass was the sword mainly used by sailors. Blade length 50-60 cm, weight 1.2-1.4 kg, width 3-5 cm. The cutlass, which had the same form as the early hanger, was widely used by sailors around the 18th century. On warships and pirate ships it was an important weapon for boarding or storming enemy vessels.
The most commonly available blade, used by Slayers before they take up proper blades.
