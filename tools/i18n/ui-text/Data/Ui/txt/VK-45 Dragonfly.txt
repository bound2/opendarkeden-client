The VK-45 is a compact machine gun developed for those who want a small but powerful weapon. The submachine gun, which began with the idea of "pouring out pistol rounds fast", was mainly used by special forces, but because it is light and easy to carry it became the main weapon of Slayers who must hunt Vampires in secret. As the first SMG modified to fire VK rounds, it made great strides in power, and with a sturdy stock and a specially treated barrel it offers great versatility for its low price.
