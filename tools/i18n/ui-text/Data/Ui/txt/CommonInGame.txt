Press Tab to open the Inventory window, then press Ctrl+Tab and drag items onto the gear window to equip them.
To attack a monster, point the mouse at it and left-click.
STR affects Damage, HP and Protection.
DEX affects To Hit and Defense.
INT affects MP, SP and certain spells of Clerics and Ousters.
Every item has stat requirements.
Click a monster's corpse after killing it and its head drops (a gem in the Teen version).
Monster heads (gems in the Teen version) can be exchanged for money at each guild's NPC.
Areas shown in green on the minimap are safe zones.
Skills cannot be used in safe zones.
To trade, hold the left Alt key in a safe zone and click the player you want to trade with.
Attacking your own race worsens your Alignment.
If you keep repeating the same sentence in chat, you will be unable to chat for a while.
Items can be obtained by killing monsters on any map.
To form a party, hold the right Alt key and click the player you want to party with.
When the chat text is red you cannot talk; you can talk again after 10 seconds.
Normal chat is Control+C.
Zone chat is Control+Z.
To whisper, press Control+W, enter the other player's ID and type your message.
Press Control+M to turn the minimap on or off.
Press Control+X to enter special characters.
Press Control+E to enlarge the chat window.
If you die with an Evil Alignment, you drop random items you are carrying.
Attacking a player of your own race whose Alignment is better than yours worsens your Alignment.

Vampires and monsters gain attack power and defense at night.
HP : the stat that shows your character's life. When HP reaches 0 you die.
To Hit (TOHIT) : the stat that shows attack accuracy; it depends on DEX and on skill and Domain levels.
Damage (DAMAGE) : the stat that shows how hard you can hit; it depends on STR and skill level.
Defense (DEFENSE) : the stat that shows your chance to evade against To Hit; it depends on DEX.
Protection (PROTECTION) : the stat that shows how much Damage you shrug off; it depends on STR.
Fame (FAME) : rises as you kill monsters or complete quests.
When your character figure appears at the top left of the screen, items shown in yellow or red need repairing.
Every item shows its stat requirements.
Options do not apply on equipped items shown in red.
If someone attacks you first, the self-defense system applies.
While self-defense is active, you can strike back for a while without affecting your Alignment.
Looting the corpse of a player whose Alignment is better than yours lowers your Alignment.
You can choose between Enter chat and Normal chat in the Options.
Interfaces such as the chat window and map can auto-hide. If hovering the mouse over the button shows 'Turn auto-hide on', auto-hide is currently off.
Interfaces such as the chat window and map can be made translucent. If hovering the mouse over the button shows 'Display translucent.', the window is currently opaque.
Shift-click an item in your inventory to move it to the gear window.
When the mouse is at the edge of the chat window, the pointer turns into a hand; click and drag to resize the chat window.
Hacking must be reported in the hacking report section of the Customer Support Center within 5 days of the incident.
Click the green circle on the HP bar to resize the HP bar.
Click the red circle on the HP bar to switch the HP bar between horizontal and vertical.
If you turned from a Vampire back into a Slayer through an NPC, you must wait one week in real time and then have your blood drained to become a Vampire again.
You can set every hotkey to suit you in the Options window.
With Caps Lock on, hold down the left or right mouse button to automatically attack enemies within attack range around you.
