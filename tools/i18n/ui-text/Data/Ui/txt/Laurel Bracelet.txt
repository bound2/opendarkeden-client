A splendid bracelet for Slayers, its outer pattern showing laurel leaves, which signify victory, peace and immortality. In Greek myth the laurel is said to be the water nymph 'Daphne', transformed after she refused Apollo's love. The bracelet is made of gold and a black ore, and because the laurel drawn on it is said to grant the dead peace and rest, it was also used in funeral rites.
