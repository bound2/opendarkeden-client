This circlet is made from the roots and dried leaves of the walnut tree. The tree has a poor reputation because witches danced around it at their gatherings. Walnut roots secrete a substance called juglone that kills the plants growing nearby. Resting in the shade of a walnut tree is very dangerous, as it drains your life energy, so be careful. Putting a few walnut leaves under your pillow gives you dreams that show the future, so you can avoid danger.
