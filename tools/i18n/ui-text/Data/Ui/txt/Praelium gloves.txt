Ousters chosen as warriors to go to the battlefield had to abandon their usual bright, clear air and bury themselves in darkness. Their garb is uniformly a dark purple, the color of death. Purple signifies death but also a noble soul, and it was a declaration of their will to give themselves wholly to the god of death. They believe that if they meet death on the battlefield, their purple garments will become their shrouds and protect their souls from harm.
