A bracelet engraved with an unusual pattern. The picture is carved in relief and depicts the war between the race of light and the race of darkness before the creation of the world, with the race of darkness defeating the race of light as its theme. Look closely and you can see a scene of the 'Blood Rite' that marks the beginning and end of the war. The offering in the rite was the pure blood of a virgin taken on the night of the full moon, and it is told that her body was cut to pieces and fed to wild animals, wolves above all.
