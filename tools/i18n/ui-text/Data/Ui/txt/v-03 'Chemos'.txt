Russian snipers needed a new bolt-action sniper rifle capable of precise long-range fire. So in 1998 Vladimir Stronskiy designed the Snaiperskaya Vintovka for the Arms R&D department of Izhmash OJSC, and there the V-03 'Chmos' sniper rifle was made. The barrel and chamber are cold-rolled, and each bore is finished after forging. The barrel is fixed to the receiver with four pins, and the barrel and receiver are fixed to the stock with two bolts. It also mounts a PKS-07 scope with a wide 7x range.
