A ring with a gilded line through the middle. As the word 'gilt' says, it is simply a ring wrapped in gold, but it also carries the sense of 'guilt', so it is also called the 'Ring of Sin'. It is said that when you put it on, an evil energy runs up your hand and through your whole body. A red energy, linked to the color of blood, circulates through the body, and the eyes and lips turn reddish at the same time. A sadistic streak takes hold as well, so that during Blood Drain the wearer torments the victim and drinks the blood at the peak of it.
