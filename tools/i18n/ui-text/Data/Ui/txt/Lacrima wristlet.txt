A wristlet that renders in a modern style the image of Chaos, the state of disorder before all things in the universe came into being. It can be a symbol for today's young people, who live their daily lives amid anxiety and fear, the chaos of the modern age. The circle of chaos is a passage through which matter from outside the universe enters our world, a substance that quite literally summons chaos. The wristlet carries the wish that young people living through the turmoil of chaos may find their own path.
