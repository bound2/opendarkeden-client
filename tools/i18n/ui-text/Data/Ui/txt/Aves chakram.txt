As a throwing and striking weapon, the Chakram's basic shape was bound by its function, so the Ousters began improving it in other ways: adorning it lavishly with gems, reshaping parts of it, or adding patterns imbued with spells. The Aves Chakram is a Chakram decorated with chalcedony agate. Agate has been prized in East and West since ancient times, and ancient ornaments made of it are found all over the world. It falls broadly into two kinds: chalcedony, whose color is fairly even, and banded agate. Of these, chalcedony was thought to be a powerful amulet. Greek sailors of the 3rd and 4th centuries wore chalcedony amulets around their necks to keep from drowning. In the 18th century, people believed chalcedony warded off ghosts at night, just as garlic wards off vampires.
