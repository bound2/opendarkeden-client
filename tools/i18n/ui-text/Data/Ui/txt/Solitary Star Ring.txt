Of the seven kinds of symbols that make up the Greater Key of Solomon, this is the seal belonging to Mercury. Mercury is the star of Hermes in Greece and Mercurius in Rome. Interestingly, this god Mercurius/Hermes is also the god of swindlers and thieves. Indeed, Hermes is famous for having stolen Apollo's cattle on the very day he was born. So it is said that using this talisman, which borrows Mercury's power, lets one see through the hidden thoughts of others. It is also said to help one gain understanding and wisdom about all things, and many people long to own it.
