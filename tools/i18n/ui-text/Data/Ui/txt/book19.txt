Vampires are revenants, dead souls who have 'returned into the flesh', not ghosts or demons. They leave their graves at night and suck the blood of the living, going on existing after death, and their victims too become vampires after death and go out in search of new victims.

Although they are undead monsters, they were originally human, so outwardly they look almost no different from people. They are generally said, however, to have long, hideously curved fingernails, hair on their palms, taut red skin, and blood dripping from their mouths. Vampires also cast no reflection in a mirror. They do not decay even weeks after burial, and their hair grows abnormally. Their eyebrows are bushy and hair grows even on their palms. Romanian vampires sometimes have a short, hair-covered tail, and it was believed that this tail, which swells when they are angry, holds supernatural power.

Some say that most vampires prefer not to bite their victims but to suck blood through the pores of the skin. Small holes near graves are taken as further evidence: people believed that a vampire turned into mist and slipped out of its grave through them. Vampires several hundred years old are usually alone, but newly made vampires often appear in groups.
