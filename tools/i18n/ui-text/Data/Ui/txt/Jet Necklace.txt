A necklace set with jet. Jet is a gem symbolising sorrow and mourning for the dead. Vampires who have lived for ages are said to know when they will die. So before dying they leave their successor their blood necklace as proof that he is their 'successor'. Strangely, when the red blood of the dead touches this necklace, it turns white. The white wave pattern carved in its center is said to be both the symbol of 'blood' and the 'tears' of those who mourn the dead.
