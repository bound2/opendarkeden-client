The first Kahraman armor set is said to have been restored by Baron Simmons, a great 18th-century merchant and collector of ancient weapons, from pieces of armor found all along the Mediterranean coast. Once its value became known, armor of the same kind in excellent condition kept turning up. It is thought to have been worn by warriors of the medieval Ottoman Turkish Empire, and the ancient script engraved below the knee guards is wrapped in a special cloth to keep its power from running wild. The magic within is said to fill its wearer with endless strength and courage on the battlefield.
