A necklace with a red cross made of copper. The surface of the cross is the red of Christ's holy blood. Red holds a magic power of its own, and its strength varies with the person who uses it. The distinctive trait of the Cross Pendant is that the pendant itself can choose its master. If the user is stronger than the power within the pendant, it can be used at the user's will, but in the opposite case the user acts at the pendant's will. It is said it may even run wild.
