Unknown to the general public, secret factories that produce nothing but anti-vampire weapons on commission from the Holy See stand on the outskirts of Rome, Italy. The ZIG is the gun proudly launched by one of them, I&S. I&S engineers judged that although an SMG may lack an AR's firepower in the field, against Vampires the combination of an SMG's high rate of fire and the power of VK rounds would be highly effective, and luckily their prediction proved right. It could land more of the special VK rounds on fast-moving Vampires, and because it was easy to carry and had far less recoil than an AR, it made subduing Vampires easy.
