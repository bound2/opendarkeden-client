Known as "the cursed masterpiece", the Moderato is a rifle I&S produced only in limited numbers. It is 1.5 times longer than other SRs and is famous as a custom order I&S built for renowned Slayer snipers. It easily outperforms the Holt, famed for its accuracy and range. Belying its name, Moderato, its performance swings from one extreme to the other depending on each sniper's tuning skill. But all its owners died mysterious deaths and I&S's chief designer Hanson also vanished, earning it the infamous name of the cursed masterpiece.
