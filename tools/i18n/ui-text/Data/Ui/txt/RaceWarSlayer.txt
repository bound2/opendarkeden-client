Kaiser Heinrich
To the anti-vampire units of E.V.E...
We have received intelligence that this Sunday at 19 : 00 the Vampires and Ousters will wage a large-scale war over the Blood Bible in Adam's Holy Land. According to this intelligence, the Vampires' ability to operate day and night in this region was not due to any change in their bodies; something in this region was influencing them, and that something has turned out to be the Blood Bible said to lie within Adam's Holy Land. We have also found solid evidence that gathering all of the Blood Bible will let us reach their very heart. This operation is therefore a crucial chance to achieve our goal.\n\nThe combined Vampire and Ousters forces are estimated at around 250 at most. All-out war with them cannot be avoided. The outlook is grim. But we will be remembered as proud Slayer warriors who gave their all and fought to the end. Even if we all fall... we will fight not for the Vatican, the Rosenkreuz Order or the US National Security Agency that sent us here, but for ourselves. Men... I wish you luck in battle...\n\nThose who will join the war, sign up with Eileen in Octavus Castle or Judith in Septimus Castle in Adam's Holy Land...
