The Arapa worm, which lives in the Castalo highlands, is also called the vanity worm because it produces deep blue gems. It spits out one deep blue gem each time it passes an instar. These worms normally grow as larvae for more than 12 years before pupating, but it is said the Ousters can use the souls of the dead as a medium to bring them to the pupal stage in an instant. Their pupae are said to hold the power to restore lost bodily vigor.
