The Zweihander originated in Germany and is said to have first appeared around the 13th century. Its heyday ran from the mid-15th to the late 16th century, and Joseph Swetnam and George Silver, fencing masters devoted to the Elizabethan court, left written records of how to use it. To master the Zweihander one must have no physical defects, and keen eyesight and hearing are also required. The masters also stress being able to hold the sword in one hand, and training in daily life by lifting and swinging heavy objects so as to excel not only in arm strength but in agility as well. Doing so, they say, lets one eventually create techniques of one's own.
