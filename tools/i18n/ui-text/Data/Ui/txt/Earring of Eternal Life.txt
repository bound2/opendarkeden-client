An earring symbolising the eternal life of the Vampires. A pendulum of time is drawn on the medallion hanging below, and when the sun sets and darkness falls, the nine gems set in the medallion begin to give off a faint light. Each gem holds the soul of one of the dark stars, the guardian spirits of the ancient demon lord, and an ominous curse always follows this earring. Even fellow Vampires are very reluctant to go near a Vampire who wears it.
