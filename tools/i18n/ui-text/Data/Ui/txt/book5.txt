1. ASANBOSAM     A vampire found in Africa, it lives deep in the mountains and is said to meet hunters there often. It looks like an ordinary person, but its teeth and legs are made of iron and shaped like hooks. Anyone who passed by where it lived was scooped up by its legs and killed.
2. BRAHMAPARUSH  These vampires of India like to take human form. They drink their victim's blood through the skull and eat the brain, and finally wrap the victim's intestines around their own bodies and perform a ritual dance.
3. CHORDEWA      The CHORDEWA, a witch found among the ORAONS, puts her soul into a vampire cat. It is said that anyone whose lips this cat licks will soon die.
4. DEARG-DUE  In Ireland it is called the "RED BLOOD SUCKER". The ancient vampires of Celtic times were greatly feared, and the way to stop them was to pile stones on the graves believed to be theirs.
5. ERETICA  The ERETICA, a Russian vampire, is usually regarded as a heretic returned from death. They are generally known as women who sold their souls, and they come back in the shape of old women in rags. It is said that anyone who meets their gaze slowly sinks into death.
GAYAL  A vampire of India: one who dies without anyone performing the burial rites becomes a GAYAL. They are said to return seeking vengeance on their relatives or children, and to be safe from them one must perform proper funeral rites for the one who became a GAYAL.
JIGARKHWAR  These are female vampire sorcerers found in the SIND region of India. By staring fixedly or casting various spells, they draw people's livers out of their bodies. As soon as the victim dies, they cook the liver over a fire and eat it.
KUANG-SHI  A Chinese vampire, born of the violent subconscious lurking in the mind of the dead. They appear and terrify people with new techniques taught them by older KUANG-SHI.
LAMIA  A female vampire of Libya in North Africa. According to legend, a queen of Libya incurred the hatred of the goddess Hera and was killed, and in revenge she goes about devouring small children. They are also said to seduce men and devour them in a hideous form.
MASAN  A vampire of India called 'the ghost of children'. They delight in tormenting and killing small children and can cast spells on children who walk over their shadows. They are also said to follow home any woman who trails her garment over their shadow.
NOSFERATU  A Romanian vampire, created when a person born out of wedlock fathers another child out of wedlock. Set on a path of destruction as soon as they are buried, they enjoy tormenting people and holding wild drinking orgies. They can have children, and they hate newlyweds.
PELESIT  These Malaysian vampires enter people's bodies and bring sickness and death. While the evil spirit clings to them, the victims scream at the top of their lungs and rave wildly.
RAKSHASA  The RAKSHASA, a vampire of India, lures men to their deaths in the form of a beautiful woman. They live in trees, draw in lost travelers, make them vomit and cripple them. A child who eats a human brain turns into one.
VARACOLACI  A Romanian vampire of very great power. It draws strength from solar and lunar eclipses and looks like a person with pale, dry skin. They travel along star-shaped threads, and by a thread that never breaks they can travel as far as they wish.
ZMEU  The ZMEU, a vampire found in Moldavia, takes the form of fire and enters the rooms of girls and widows. Once inside, the fire turns into a man and seduces the woman.
