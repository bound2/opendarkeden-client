A vampire in werewolf form: the shape a Captain takes one day after a werewolf drains its blood. Normally, someone bitten by a werewolf changes at every full moon, but when a vampire is bitten by a werewolf it keeps that form forever, an unusual case indeed.

They show many wild, bestial traits. Some scholars studying the Alcan have gone so far as to define it as 'a werewolf with high intelligence'.
