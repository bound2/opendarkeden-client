A wristlet made of orichalcum, known as the metal of fantasy. Orichalcum is a metal from the legends of Atlantis, said to be the strongest of metals or a mysterious metal holding immense energy, and it is thought to be linked to the philosopher's stone sought by medieval alchemists. Orichalcum is a transparent metal, deeply magical, the very essence of magic power. Yet only its own inherent magic can be used; no one knows any way to draw out whatever other power it hides.
