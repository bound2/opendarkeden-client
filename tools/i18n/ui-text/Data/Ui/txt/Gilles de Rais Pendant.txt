The ancient Vampire Masters long searched for a way to store the magic power that overflowed from them beyond control. Then, in the 2nd century BC, the Egyptian alchemist Marcus created a new metal from which magic power could be freely stored and drawn. Hoping to gain eternal life and magic power, he presented necklaces to the Vampire Masters. But Marcus, who guarded his secrets and grew too greedy, lost his life at the hands of Master Chaspa. The method of making these necklaces has been passed down among the Masters for generations, and to those who study magic it is known as a material more precious than gold.
