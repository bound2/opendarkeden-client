Coiled in this somewhat intricate form is the ouroboros serpent, which among the key symbols of alchemy stands for its core concepts and beliefs. Its strange shape also resembles a flower in bloom, so it is sometimes called the Blossom. The ouroboros (a serpent biting its own tail), a very old symbol expressing the heart of alchemical philosophy, conveys the unity of matter far better than the writings of any alchemical master. The symbol here does not take the classic ouroboros form; it combines the ouroboros serpent with a rather intricate knot, adding the meaning of an unbreakable knot so that it becomes a symbol of eternal bond and oneness.
