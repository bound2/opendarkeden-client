A shotgun made by SPAS that has proven its worth with French counter-terrorism units, among others. It is easy to operate and can switch flexibly between semi-automatic and pump action depending on the ammunition and firepower needed, making it the very model of the modern shotgun. However, its complex internals make it prone to jamming and hard to repair. Coveted by anyone easily intoxicated by the smell of gunpowder, this gun has the nickname 'Thug'.
