A kind of ancient Sumerian or Akkadian spirit. Spirits in general are all called utukku, and the good ones among them are called lamassu. They have human form but sometimes the head of a bird or beast, and are said to have four bird's wings on their backs. Beings much like the angels of Christianity, they acted as intermediaries between gods and humans. They also served as guardian deities of humanity, leading people in the right direction and protecting them from the evil spirits and calamities that assail them.
