A ring symbolizing Volasio, one of the fourteen martyred saints whose names Christians call on to intercede for them in danger and hardship. These fourteen saints come from the legend that, facing persecution and a gruesome death, they asked God for special favor, and their veneration began in central Germany around the 13th century and spread across all of Europe. In art these saints are usually represented by animals or objects; for Volasio it is 'two candles in the shape of a cross'.
