The Battalion was originally to be released as the P-3000, the latest product of a famous Russian small-arms design bureau. But its firepower was excessive for an anti-personnel weapon, and its approval was held up for years, leaving it on the verge of being shelved. For EVE, however, which faces the unusual foe that is the Vampires, that firepower was an attraction, and a small number were acquired with the tacit consent of international bodies.
With its integrated TA/FCS system, it can spot targets at night or in bad weather, calculate the figures needed for aiming and show them on the user's terminal.
