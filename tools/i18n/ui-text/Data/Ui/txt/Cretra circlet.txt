The symbol of the Golden Dawn, one of the West's esoteric religious societies; it makes a Circlet for creative will and focus of mind. This symbol is the emblem of the mystical society called the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn. The order was the most influential of the modern Western magical and mystical societies, and many mystical groups based on it still exist today. The Golden Dawn took a great interest in ancient myths and symbols, and many ancient elements found their way into its own symbolism. The symbol shown here is essentially a combination of the winged sun disc, symbol of the Egyptian god Aten, and the sacred serpent, the uraeus.
