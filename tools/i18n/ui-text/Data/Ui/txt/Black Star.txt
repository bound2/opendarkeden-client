The Black Star is an event item that can be exchanged with a GM for a family photo voucher.

Use: To celebrate May, the month of the family, it can be exchanged for a family photo voucher (gift certificate) so your family can take a warm photo together. Keep it until May 15 and hand it in to a GM, and the family photo voucher will be delivered to your home. Keep it safe, and make some good memories with your family.

GM in charge of exchanges: GM1
