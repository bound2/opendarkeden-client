The Pungo Mace is a fearsome weapon that crushes bone and mangles flesh with the heavy iron ball and sharp spikes on its end. It is said to have been made by some monks of the medieval Pachomian monastery who held misguided beliefs. In the false name of Jehovah's will, they cruelly executed anyone who went against the faith they had set up, and the people who died at their hands under false accusations were beyond counting. Their atrocities, which lasted 150 years, were uncovered in 1590 and ended with their agonizing deaths.
