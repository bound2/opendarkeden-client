Creatures such as the Ungoliant, a kind of spider that takes the form of a beautiful human woman, are mutants who break the order of nature, and so the Ousters also took on the role of destroying them. Commonly called spider-women, they were ordinary spiders about 2 centimeters long found all over ancient Eslania. Transformed into beautiful women, the Ungoliants often seduced men and made them their prey; they were extremely ferocious and had eternal life, so the only way to destroy them was to plate them alive in gold or some other hard metal. Such plated Ungoliants still turn up now and then, and people make them into accessories in honor of the valor of their ancestors.
