Arms dealers like to spin a tale that this helmet belonged to a French noble in the Hundred Years' War, but despite its looks it is the latest combat helmet made by Germany's HAC. Modified for use against Vampires, its shock-resistant special ABS shell is plated in silver and then finished in gold. It is fitted with special equipment such as a virtual reality display, a GPS receiver, a 360-degree observation video camera, night-vision gear, headphones and a voice-recognition microphone.
