A cast spell that damages every enemy approaching you for a set time; damage is determined by INT and skill level. Duration is determined by INT and skill level.

A skill that summons the Salamander, a middle fire spirit, to attack the enemy. The Salamander, made of fire, guards the caster's body and attacks approaching enemies with flame. A ring of fire surrounds the caster's body and damages enemies that come near. You can use other skills while it is active.
