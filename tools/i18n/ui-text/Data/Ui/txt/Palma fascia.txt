A sash worn by the shrine maiden Palma, who served Carissas in the early days of the Ousters race. No precise records of Palma remain. She is said to have greatly helped Carissas gain the power to heal Vampires' victims, yet never revealed herself to anyone else; all that is told is that she was originally one of the Vampire race and, for unknown reasons, came to serve Carissas. Her sash is said to have held a spiritual power that kept the spirits from feeling any aversion to her, a Vampire.
