Around 1920, a Slayer discovered an ancient ruin. Inside were found a giant skeleton and a lump of iron that seemed to be a sword hilt; on the corpse's neck and wrist were a necklace and bracelet strung from fangs, which EVE presumed to be Vampire fangs. The lump of iron was engraved all over with unknown images, and from it and the little that remained of the blade, EVE succeeded in reconstructing the sword. The reconstructed sword was so big and heavy that even well-trained Slayers could barely lift it. The pattern on the hilt was copied without ever being deciphered, and EVE put the sword into mass production, but only a small number were made.
