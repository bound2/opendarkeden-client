The pentacle is a symbol the Pythagoreans stamped on their letters like a seal, wishing their friends good health. A pentacle is a five-pointed star that can be drawn without once lifting the pen. It was used in many cultures, and in ancient Greece in particular it was also the symbol of Hygieia, goddess of health. So the pentacle is called the sign of Hygieia (signum Hygeae) or the sign of health (signum Salutatis). What you see here is a circlet engraved with the pentagram, Hygieia's symbol, used as a talisman.
