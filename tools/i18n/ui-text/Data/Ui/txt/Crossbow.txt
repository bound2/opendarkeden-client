A bomb packed with a large amount of C4; as crude as it looks, it boasts high destructive power. Because of its shape it is also called the 'stick grenade', and though it flies far and hits its target accurately, it has the drawbacks of being heavy and awkward to carry. A grenade used by the German army: you open the safety cap and pull the firing cord with its ring-shaped weight, which sets off the fuse by friction. Since this grenade produces few fragments, a metal fragmentation sleeve is sometimes fitted to the warhead.
