A ring of black ore, shaped like a twisted cord. Set in its center is a translucent stone called cat's eye, and the ring goes by that name too. A cat's eye signifies the waxing and waning of the moon and the radiance of the night. This cat's eye, a raw stone shaped like a cat's eye, has the peculiarity that red ancient letters appear inside it at night. It is used as a charm for long life and protection.
