A wristlet shaped like a raven's wings. In Celtic myth the raven is the symbol of strife, war and death, and is tied to the wisdom needed to overcome them. To the ancient Celts, animals were especially sacred. They made many animal symbols for religious purposes or simply as ornaments, and many such symbols can still be seen in surviving Celtic tombs. The raven is a symbol of strife, war and death. Its wisdom is a wisdom joined with trickery. The raven is also the guardian of sacred records. Objects bearing the raven's symbol serve as talismans and are believed to help their owner attain the highest wisdom.
