The acidic substance secreted inside Vampires' bodies has always been a dreaded threat that halves Slayer morale. To counter it, EVE set out to create a new combat suit and, as a trial, developed combat boots using the material of Jupon's space suits. Made of five layers of aluminum-coated special fiber with a layer of polyester nonwoven fabric between them and a Teflon coating, these boots guarantee top mobility while serving admirably as flawless protective gear. Pleased with their performance in the field, EVE reportedly plans to spare no support for developing combat suits of the same material.
