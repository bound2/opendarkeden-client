The Minerva, Karastoyev's second creation, succeeded the POLAN: its heavy barrel improved stability, and its ergonomically designed parts made shooting more comfortable. Above all, this gun's great strength is its improved accuracy. Accurate enough to snipe with nothing more than a scope fitted, it gave Karastoyev the confidence to go on to develop sniper guns as well. Naturally, the destructive power of its high-spinning bullets is also excellent, and many Slayers use it.
