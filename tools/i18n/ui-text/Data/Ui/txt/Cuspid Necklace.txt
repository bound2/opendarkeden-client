A necklace bearing a blood-soaked fang. A pendant born of a blood pact, it is made after one becomes a Vampire by pulling a canine tooth from the first human whose blood one drained and soaking it in that person's blood. A necklace made in celebration, it is called the 'Necklace of Blood' or the 'Necklace of Blessing', marking one's birth as a Vampire.
