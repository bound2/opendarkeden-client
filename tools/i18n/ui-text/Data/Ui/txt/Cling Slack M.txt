Combat wear developed by gathering the requests of fighters who had served long in E.V.E.; thanks to new materials it is known to be light and flexible. But since it is not the product of an official military supplier, ordinary fighters have been forbidden to use it. Veterans, however, knew that official armor alone was not enough to stand against ever stronger enemies and favored this product, and the leadership, judging that it could not keep suppressing the demands of seasoned warriors, eventually granted limited permission to use it.
