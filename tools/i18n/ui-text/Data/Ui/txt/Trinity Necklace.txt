A Slayer necklace with four purple beads surrounding a central circle. It symbolizes the Christian Trinity, and it was long thought to have been made in the Vatican, the seat of Catholicism, but documents recently revealed it to be the seventh work of the 17th-century St. Ardicuas. St. Ardicuas is said to have made exactly 49 sets filled with his blessing and given them to devout believers.
