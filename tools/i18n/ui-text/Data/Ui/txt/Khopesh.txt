The khopesh is an Egyptian sword with a grip about 6 inches long. Its shape is very distinctive. Looking overall like a single-bladed axe drawn out long, it has an 18-inch straight section joined to a blade of about 2 feet that gradually narrows. Blade and hilt are formed in one piece, and it looks rather like a fodder chopper.
Usually made of bronze or iron, it is heavy, awkward to use and very slow. It fits into no other class of sword, so to use it you must learn techniques made for this weapon alone.
