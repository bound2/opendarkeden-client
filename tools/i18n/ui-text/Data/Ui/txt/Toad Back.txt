Equipment originally developed for a heavy infantry unit that EVE was planning. It is quite bulky, but since it is actually made from many layers of pressed fiber, it is fairly light. It was made to protect the shoulders, which face the enemy during a charge, and the studs on its surface can also be used for attacking with powerful tackles. However, it is not easy to tackle Vampires and Ousters, who move faster than humans. Because it resembles a toad's bumpy back, it is called the 'Toad Back'.
