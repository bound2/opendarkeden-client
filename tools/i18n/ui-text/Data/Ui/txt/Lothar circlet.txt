A circlet of platinum decorated with amber, a mysterious gem. Because amber produces electricity through friction, its Greek name, elektron, gave us the word for electricity. Amber is one of the finest decorative gems humanity has ever used. In the West, relics carved in many ways by cave dwellers have been found in Egypt and Switzerland. Amber was one of the first gem materials used as a talisman, and amber carved into animal shapes was held the most potent, so people wore it to ward off witches. Amber jewelry was also placed in graves to ensure a happy afterlife, which is why this circlet is often unearthed from ancient tombs and ruins.
