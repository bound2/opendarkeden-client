A Vampire ring shaped like a loop of several skulls joined together. It is said to be made on the night of a full moon, when the moon's energy is strongest, by digging up the grave of a corpse dead exactly one month, taking out the skull and carving it into a ring. In legend this ring was used to summon the dead, so it is also called the 'Ring of Summoning'. Made of human bone, the ring breaks after a certain period of use. That is why, on the day after a full moon, the graveyards are said to be dug up like a kicked beehive by Vampires making rings.
