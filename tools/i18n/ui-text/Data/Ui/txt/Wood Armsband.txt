A kind of spirit that has existed since ancient times in the form of a tree. Its branches are its hands and its roots its feet, so the number of limbs and the color of the skin differ from tree to tree. They are said to have been able to walk about, and they are called Ents. Today, out of fear of humans, they reveal their true form only to a few other races. Close friends of the Ousters, they give the Ousters bark from their ancient trunks, grown over the ages, helping them stay always under nature's protection.
