A high-end model among the improved MKs, the G2000 has a somewhat complex internal design and slightly higher accuracy. Built on the mechanism of the "GOOSE", with tuning said to have been done by Karastoyev, this gun showed astonishing destructive power, able to suppress a Vampire's counterattack with just a few rounds. However, because of its many high-end parts it was never widely issued, and Karastoyev later parted ways with the MK series to develop his own models.
