Having handled almost every gun there is, Slayers who chose the rifle as their main weapon held the MK series to be the best: reliable at life-or-death moments and able to shatter a Vampire's head with one shot. To meet this explosive popularity, an astonishing gun that had passed even harsher field testing was released: the "GOOSE", praised as the best value for money in the MK series.
This gun is heavier than the MK-74, so its recoil is relatively lower and its accuracy in automatic fire is dramatically improved. It has night sights that make aiming possible even on dark nights, a folding stock and a flash hider designed for sVK rounds, but above all the appeal of the "GOOSE" lies in being able to fire freely the reinforced 7.62mm sVK round, made to stop Vampires who do not fall to ordinary sustained fire.
