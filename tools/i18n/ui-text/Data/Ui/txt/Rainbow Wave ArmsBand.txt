An ancient form of the 'Menorah', the chief symbol of Judaism, a religious symbol that helps one draw greater wisdom from the energy of nature. This symbol, which gives strength especially to those resolved to live each day in a more ordered and wiser way, has been made into the shape of armor. The Menorah is a great seven-branched candlestick, and the candlestick symbolizes the tree of light. It is said that its light shines up to the Lord, and all other lights shine toward it. First, the seven branches represent the sun, the moon and the five planets Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn and Mars, and at the same time the seven days of the week. It may be seen as depicting the whole universe and human life. The three U shapes used in the candlestick symbolize wisdom, strength and beauty respectively.
