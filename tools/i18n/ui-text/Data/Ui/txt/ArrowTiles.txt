Arrow Tiles
Guide your character from the START point to the GOAL point while staying clear of the monsters.
Press the START button to begin the game.
Press the MOVE button and your character moves in the direction of the arrow it is standing on.
After each move you may turn the arrows clockwise or counterclockwise, once. Note that all arrows turn the same way at the same time.
Every turn, each monster moves one tile in the direction of its arrow.
On a blue arrow you slide straight on. On a red arrow you lose a turn.
You fail if you run into a monster or can no longer move.
