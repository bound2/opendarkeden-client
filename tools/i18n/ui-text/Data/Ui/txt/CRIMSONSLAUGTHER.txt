Wielding chainsaws, they live up to their name, slaughtering anyone in their path, and are also known as 'the Butchers'. They are said to think nothing of cruelty such as catching people as they flee and driving a chainsaw into their necks to sever their heads.

At the sight of red blood they fall into a frenzy and claim even more victims; wherever they pass, countless headless corpses can be seen.
