Armor refers to the protective gear Slayers wear: helmets, bulletproof vests, gloves, trousers, shoes and so on. The attributes to look at carefully when buying armor are Defense and Protection. Defense is the defensive attribute set against the opponent's To Hit, and Protection is the one set against the opponent's Damage. In other words, high Defense means you are rarely hit by attacks, and high Protection means that even when you are hit, the damage is greatly reduced. Bear in mind, though, that armor high in both is very expensive.
