The first to marry Rudra, the god of destruction, was the goddess Sati. But Sati died, promising to be reborn and find him again, and Rudra, grieving the loss of his wife, gave himself over to nothing but austerities. Sati was reborn as Palvati, but Rudra, still absorbed in his austerities, would not even open his eyes.
Meanwhile the world of the gods came under threat from a mighty demon, and the gods learned that the only one who could destroy it was a child born to Rudra and Palvati.
After many twists and turns, Shiva at last realized that she was Sati reborn and married her, and the child she bore finally defeated the demon, bringing peace to the society of the gods.
