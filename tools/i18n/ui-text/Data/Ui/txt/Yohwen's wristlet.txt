A wristlet depicting the snake bird, the Native American symbol that commands the sun to rise. It combines a bird and a snake into a unique symbol, 'a snake with feathers'. The snake bird has black feathers and a rain-shaped beard. In Native American myth this snake is a kind of saviour, and because it commands the sun to rise and herald the bright start of morning, it is also called 'the Lord of Dawn'. The Lord of Dawn will deliver the sun's energy for a fresh morning without oversleeping.
