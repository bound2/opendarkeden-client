- Vampire legends of Mexico
In Mexico there are tales of a vampire called the Cihuateteo, or Cihuapipiltin. Women who died in childbirth are said to become this vampire; it can fly, and it gives children fits or fevers. It usually appears in the middle of crossroads, where it holds demonic feasts and summons the dead. The Cihuateteo is also said to own a magic broom, and her clothes are painted with skulls. She is a dangerous being who attacks anyone she meets at night without warning.
- Vampire legends of Arabia
Arabian vampires are almost always female and live in lonely huts. Half human and half demon, this vampire appears in graveyards at night, digs up freshly made graves and feeds on the flesh and blood of the dead. Or, finding a lost traveler, she cunningly bewitches him, tears at his flesh and drinks his blood. Strangely, this vampire can take human form and even bear children. Among the tales of "The Arabian Nights" is the story of a vampire caught by her husband while drinking the blood of the dead.
