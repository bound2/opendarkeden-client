The PG-13 'Roc' can be called the most accurate semi-automatic sniper rifle in the world. Its accuracy is such that at 300 meters a 50-round group lands inside an 80 mm (3.14") circle. The PG-13 'Roc' is popular with a few special units and select counterterrorist units. It suits a military environment for two reasons: first, it ejects spent casings about 10 meters away, and second, it carries a fixed Hensoldt 6x42 scope. The 6x is fixed for use at 600 meters, because in combat military snipers position themselves around 600-700 meters away. The PG-13 'Roc' combines the strengths of the single-shot sniper rifle and the semi-automatic rifle, making it the finest sniper rifle for police and counterterrorist units, but it is so expensive that only the most elite units have it.
