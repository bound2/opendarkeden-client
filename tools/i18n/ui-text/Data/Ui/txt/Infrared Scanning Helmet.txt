A helmet of reinforced plastic fitted with an automatic infrared scope. The infrared scope over the eyes recognizes objects by body heat and shows them on a small screen, making it useful even in the dark. It replaces the manual operation of earlier helmets with infrared scanning systems with automatic operation. That had long been recognized as a problem but was not easily fixed. It covers the face, improving defense, but its steel makes it feel heavy. The infrared sensor distinguishes objects precisely, improving accuracy against targets. Equipped with AISS (Automatic-Infrared Scanning System).
