Mace-type weapons served as effective striking weapons against heavily armored foes. But as metal armor lost its usefulness, so did the mace, and it came to be seen merely as a symbol of strength or power that played up its outward violence. The Salience Asser in the hands of a Granter likewise has an intimidating form and is not used for direct blows. Yet, being made of metal, an attribute-free substance, it is recognized as an item that faithfully serves as a medium to channel the Granters' magic power effectively.
