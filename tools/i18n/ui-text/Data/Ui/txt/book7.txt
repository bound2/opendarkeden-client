Chief among a vampire's powers is shapeshifting. It can turn into a wolf, a rat, a cat, a frog and so on, and change its size at will. This power lets it get in anywhere, and even leave its coffin without digging its way out of the grave.

Vampires also do not age. Since they are corpses it may be only natural that they do not grow older, but neither do they suffer the decay that a corpse should. Dig up their graves and you will find them lying in their coffins as if alive and asleep. Most frightening of all, having lived so long, they surpass ordinary people in knowledge and possess great strength and curses. They are also said to have such tremendous charisma that they can put members of the opposite sex into a trance.

In fact, the vampire of legend can turn not only into spiders and butterflies but into any animal at all, and even into mist or straw.
