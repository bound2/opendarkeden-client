Around the 11th century there were those who went to fight in faraway lands to defend their own convictions and the convictions of their faith. Those who came back alive split into two: those whose convictions grew even firmer, and those who had been betrayed.
This sword, used by the Crusaders (the warriors of the Crusades), also served as the 'cross', the token of their convictions. It is a sword of fitting size and weight to be handled with a shield and even on horseback, and its sleek blade delivers stable, great power. Above all, as a sword imbued with the convictions of the Crusaders, it must be a thoroughly unpleasant weapon for Vampires. It is a fine sword that adds holy power to the power of the blade.
