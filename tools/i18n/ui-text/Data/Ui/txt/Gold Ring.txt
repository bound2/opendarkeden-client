A ring of gold with unusual decoration. Set in its center are six gemstones signifying loyalty, wisdom, foresight, courage, protection from evil, healing, innocence and resurrection. Whoever wears it is therefore said to receive the power of the six stones. In order: garnet, amethyst, emerald, ruby, peridot, sapphire (said to be the ring worn by the cardinals of the Vatican) and topaz. Only the inherent powers of these six stones act on the ring, and none of them opposes another.
