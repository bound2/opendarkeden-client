A Vampire ring made of iron. Set in its center is a 'ruby', symbolising the red of blood. The red ruby represents passion; its wearer can use the ruby's red glow to seduce others into a daze and drink their blood, but it is said to work only on humans, never on Vampires. Legend says the ruby was left by a witch imprisoned in a castle long ago who, knowing her life was at an end, left behind a stone reddened with her own blood before she died, and that her soul dwells in that stone.
