A basic long-range attack spell of the Fire domain; damage increases with INT and skill level. Range depends on skill level.

A spirit spell that borrows the power of Kasa, a lesser fire spirit in the shape of a small eagle. When his small eagle forms come together, they are said to form a giant phoenix. The name comes from how Kasa's small eagle shapes fly like arrows, and Kasa, made of flame, is said to aim for the enemy's heart.
