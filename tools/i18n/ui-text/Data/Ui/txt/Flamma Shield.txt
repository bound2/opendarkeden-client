A Slayer shield shaped like two overlapping layers of solar flame, boasting defense as powerful as its splendid looks. It was found in south-eastern Mongolia but is thought to have been made by an ancient East Asian people who worshipped the sun. Shifting subtly in color with the angle of the light, it is said to be made of a material that cannot be produced under Earth's atmosphere and gravity, which only adds to its mystique.
