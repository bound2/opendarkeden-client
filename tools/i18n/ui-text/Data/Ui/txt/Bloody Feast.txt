An earring made to commemorate the Vampire festival known as the cruel June massacre. Made from the bones and blood of those who died that day, the Bloody Feast holds the grudges of 50,000 people, and whoever wears this earring, steeped in their curses and grudges, is said to be always excited and to crave Blood Drain. Its bone frame is colored with copper dye, and in the square-looking cup, clotted blood has hardened like a ruby. To those who remember that day it is an earring of terror, but Vampires collect it as a party souvenir.
