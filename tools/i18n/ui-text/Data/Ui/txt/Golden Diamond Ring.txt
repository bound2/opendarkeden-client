A ring made of gold. Unusual in appearance, it is a gold band covered with a gold wire tied in reed knots. Set in its center is a diamond, signifying light, life, the sun, permanence and immortality. These two gems of the sun are said to help in annihilating Vampires. Because it grants its wearer the power of annihilation, it is also called the 'Ring of Annihilation'.
