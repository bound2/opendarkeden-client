Desirish, the spirit of hope, is also called the last and greatest gift of the gods, kept in the Pandora's box the gods gave to mankind. It is said to give hope to those in despair so that they can overcome any hardship, and meeting this spirit is thought to be the greatest of fortunes. Making an object imbued with Desirish's energy is so hard that it would use up a whole lifetime's luck, so from ancient times to this day only a very few have ever owned one.
