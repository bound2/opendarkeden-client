The initial shotgun model released by RESS, the multinational pharmaceutical giant, after it acquired Buell, famous for its MT series; it can use VKU-2 rounds. A finished gun developed through experiments with the Alligator. Its destructive power is beyond doubt, but its reliability is still a work in progress. In close combat it shows "demonic power", but because of stability issues it is currently issued only on a limited basis.
