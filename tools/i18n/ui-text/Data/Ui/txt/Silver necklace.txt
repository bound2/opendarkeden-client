A Slayer necklace with a pendant of two white horses facing in opposite directions. Because the pendant resembles the white horses that draw the sun god's chariot in Greek myth, it is also called 'Steeds of the Heavenly Lord'. The winged horses of legend are said to rise as lightly as clouds and run as swiftly as lightning, and it is said that wearing it makes one's movements nimble. Of the two white horses on the pendant, the left one stands for death and the moon, the other for life and the sun.
