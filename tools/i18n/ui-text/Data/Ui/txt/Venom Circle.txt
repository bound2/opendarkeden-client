Since long ago, some of our kin have had the power to amplify the poison within their bodies. Those who push that limit to its peak can spread an extremely potent venom over a wide area. By the time the enemy notices the fierce, cruel storm of venom pouring from the body of one casting this terrible magic, their whole body will already have corroded away.

* Once you reach Advancement level 50, you can learn it free of charge with a skill book from the Market.
