Melia, the spirit of the ash tree, is said to have been born when the blood of the sky god fell upon the earth. In "Works and Days" the ancient Greek poet Hesiod divides the history of the humans made by the gods into five races, and the third of these, the race of bronze, is said to have actually been born from ash trees. Perhaps for this reason, some regard the ash spirit Melia as the ancestor of humankind. Today, however, with nature destroyed by humans, they can barely survive, and they are deeply afraid of humans. When the land they live in is polluted they die, and at that moment they vanish without a trace, leaving behind only the circlet they wore on their heads.
