Holy water, the by-product of Create Holy Water, the first step of the cleric's Enchant domain. It is produced in proportion to the water bottle used, and is made with a Medium Water Bottle. Holy water means "sacred water": clean water specially blessed by a priest. Used for religious purification, it signifies washing away all that is unclean by driving off the sins and demons that hinder holiness. When believers dip their fingertips in holy water and make the sign of the cross on entering a church, they do so to recall the promises made at their baptism and in the hope that their bodies and minds will be cleansed as they enter.
