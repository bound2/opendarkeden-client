A wristlet bearing a symbol derived from Tibetan calligraphy that represents samsara. In Tibetan Buddhism, samsara means 'the endlessly turning cycle of suffering'. Samsara is the flow in which all living beings repeat life and death, continuing their lives in other forms of existence. Within samsara, beings may be born not only as humans but in higher heavens, or, unluckily, as animals or in hell. Because Buddhism holds that life itself is suffering, it sees samsara, the endless repetition of life and death, as a cycle of suffering, and so seeks to escape that cycle and win eternal salvation. In Buddhist terms this salvation is called liberation, or nirvana. The samsara wristlet is a religious symbol that brings a deep realization that life is a painful round of rebirth, and so strengthens the will toward nirvana.
